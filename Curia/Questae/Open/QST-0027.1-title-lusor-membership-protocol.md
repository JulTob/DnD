## 🔍 Diagnosis (what & where)

The title map wants to select specific words with checks such as
`"Wizard" in lusor`, `"Cleric" in lusor`, and `"Lawful" in genus`, but it does
not currently trust a general interface. `Genus(lusor)` reconstructs a
character string by probing known attributes. That makes `Map_of_Titles`
depend on the internal shapes of Character and NPC objects.

## 🧾 Evidence

`Genus(lusor)` currently handles:

- strings as a special case;
- objects with `.genus`;
- fallback `getattr` checks for `race`, `subrace`, `archetype`,
  `char_class`, `gender`, and `alignment`.

Other helpers use both `"X" in genus` and `"X" in lusor`, so there is no single
membership contract.

## 🎯 Desired outcome

- `Map_of_Titles` can ask `"Characteristic" in lusor` without knowing whether
  the lusor is a string, list, set, dict, Character, NPC, or later TagKit
  composed object.
- Character/NPC objects own their own membership behavior.
- Built-in containers remain useful test inputs.
- The title map stops requiring concrete attributes from generated objects.

## 🧭 Notes for the Agora / implementer

- Decide in Dialog 0007 whether the contract is direct membership only, or a
  tiny helper such as `has_characteristic(lusor, characteristic)`.
- Keep the implementation small. Do not introduce a hierarchy or new framework
  just to support membership.
- Add `__main__` examples for string, list, set, dict, and a minimal object
  with `__contains__`.
