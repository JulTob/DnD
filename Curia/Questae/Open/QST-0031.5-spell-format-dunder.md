## 🔍 Diagnosis (what & where)
Spell/spellcasting rendering is scattered and re-implemented per call site rather than owned by one method. `Spell` (`Lodge_of_Spells.py`) has both a `__str__` (builds an HTML card) and a separate `.string` property (builds a plain-text `Entry` via `AtlasScriptum`) — two different renderers already competing for the same job. Separately, every `Spellcaster` subclass in `AtlasLusoris/Grimoire_of_Spellcasters.py` hand-builds its own HTML in its own `__str__`, each slightly differently (seen firsthand while filling in missing spell text — the same kind of markup, re-typed with small inconsistencies, at every subclass).

## 🎯 Desired outcome
`Spell.__format__(self, format_spec)` dispatches on the spec string: `f"{spell:html}"` renders the HTML card, `f"{spell:md}"` renders Markdown/plain prose, and a bare `f"{spell}"` (empty spec) keeps a sane default. One method, one place that knows how to render a spell — callers ask for the shape they want instead of the class exposing multiple same-purpose methods.

## 🧭 Notes for the Agora / implementer
- Natural to land as part of QST-0031.3 (spells are already being touched then) rather than as a fully separate pass, but keep it reviewable as its own diff/commit within that work.
- Once this exists, consider whether `Spellcaster.__str__`'s hand-built HTML (across its several subclasses) should route through it too — that's a bigger blast radius than `Spell` alone and may want its own follow-up Questa rather than folding in here silently.
