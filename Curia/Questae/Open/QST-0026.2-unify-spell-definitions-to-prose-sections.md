- A single, clear rendering contract: spell definitions are emitted as prose sections.
- All identified boxed spell-description paths migrated to the same prose strategy.
- Keep existing short stat/metadata blocks boxed where appropriate; only long spell text moves.

- Implement based on QST-0026.1 findings, not ad-hoc.
- Reuse existing prose helpers (`prose_section`, `_prose`) where possible.
- Avoid duplicating spell formatter logic in parallel functions.
