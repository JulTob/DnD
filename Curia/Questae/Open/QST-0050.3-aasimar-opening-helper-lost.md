## 🔍 Diagnosis (what & where)

`AtlasActorLudi/SpeciesKit/Aasimar/Map_of_Ideals.py` calls `_opening(...)` at **three** sites and defines it at none:

```
228:  f"{_opening(self.descent)} of {held} stands somewhere behind you.",
250:  f"{_opening(shade)} talaria sit at {place}, and anyone who notices "
268:  f"{_opening(form)} hangs over you in {self.colour}. {self.tell}",
```

`grep -n "_opening"` returns those three uses and nothing else. The helper was lost when the file was restored; the call sites survived. The file itself is **real authored source** (407 lines of Julio's prose), not a bytecode bootstrap, so only this one helper is missing.

From its three uses, `_opening` takes a noun phrase and returns it as an opening clause with the right indefinite article and capitalisation ("A choir…", "An aureole…"). That is an inference from the call sites, **not** a specification.

## 🎯 Desired outcome

Aasimar Characters generate, and their descent prose reads exactly as it did before the crisis.

## 🧭 Notes for the Agora / implementer

*Proposals, not decisions.*

- **Recover it, do not rewrite it.** `_opening` shapes a sentence the reader sees, so it is authored voice. Disassemble `.recovery-vault/species/Aasimar/Map_of_Ideals.cpython-314.pyc` and lift the original function body verbatim:
  ```
  python -c "import marshal,dis;dis.dis(marshal.loads(open('.recovery-vault/species/Aasimar/Map_of_Ideals.cpython-314.pyc','rb').read()[16:]))"
  ```
  Any string constants it holds ("A ", "An ", a vowel set) come back with it.
- **An agent-invented article helper is the wrong answer here** even though it would take two minutes and pass the tests. It would put words in Julio's mouth in a species' descent line, and nothing downstream would ever flag the substitution. If the bytecode turns out not to hold it, **stop and ask Julio for the line** rather than guessing.
- **Check the neighbours while you are in there.** `Map_of_Ideals.py` is not the only Aasimar file restored; confirm `resolution.py` and the other `Map_of_*` siblings have no similarly orphaned helper by importing the package and grepping for other bare `NameError` risks.
- **Verify with:** `summon_player(guild="Ranger", level=8, seed=7112)` and a handful of forced-Aasimar generations, reading the descent paragraph rather than only checking it does not raise.
