## 🔍 Diagnosis (what & where)
Helpers used while building a Character (gender, size, alignment, names, weapons, attacks, …) keep their own `import app.random` / `import random`. Even after rites use `char.dices`, these Maps reintroduce a foreign stream.

## 🧾 Evidence
Widespread `import app.random` under AtlasActorLudi, AtlasInventarium, AtlasPugna, AtlasNomina; title RNG already flagged in QST-0027.2.

## 🎯 Desired outcome
Every Map/helper on the Character construction path accepts an explicit `rng` (prefer `char.dices`) or `char`. No silent module RNG when called from summon / awaken. Callers pass the bag; helpers do not seed globals.

## 🧭 Notes for the Agora / implementer
Scope = Character path only. Epica / WorldBuild / scripts keep their own owners (out of this quest). Coordinate with QST-0027.2 for titles rather than duplicating that fix.
