## 🔍 Diagnosis (what & where)
Across two prior passes, 234 previously-undefined `Spell(...)` names in `Lodge_of_Spells.py` were filled in from official 2024/legacy rules text (sourced from the 5etools GitHub data repo — `spells-xphb.json` plus every other sourcebook file it carries: XGE, TCE, FTD, AI, EGW, SCC, and more). **0 remain undone.** Both homebrew spells in this bucket — `Widogast's Transmogrification (HB)` and `Freedom of the Winds (HB)` — were removed outright at Julio's call rather than given placeholder or invented text; he may reintroduce either later, as its own, separate topic, not as something "owed" from this Quest.

That fill-in work used one detection heuristic (an AST scan for `Spell(...)` calls with no `definition` argument or an empty one). It has **not** been independently re-verified, and it never checked:
- Whether any filled-in `definition` is malformed (stray unclosed HTML tags, leftover 5etools `{@tag}` markup that slipped past the converter, a table that rendered oddly).
- Whether the original 139-entry `SPELL_DATA_2024` dict's definitions (never touched by this work) are themselves complete and well-formed.
- Whether near-duplicate `Spell(...)` copies of the same name (the QST-0031.3 dedupe target) have **diverged** definitions that disagree with each other.

## 🎯 Desired outcome
Every `Spell` in the Lodge — both the dict and every constructor call — has a real, correctly-formatted `definition`. No spell sits in the Lodge with placeholder or missing text; a spell either has real content or isn't in the Lodge at all. A repeatable verification (ideally landing as part of QST-0031.4's test suite, not just a one-time manual pass) that would catch this class of gap again if it recurs.

## 🧭 Notes for the Agora / implementer
- This sits naturally **before or alongside** QST-0031.2/.3 (the dict-retirement and Lodge-dedupe work) — verifying data quality before migrating it is cheaper than migrating first and discovering rot after.
- Both homebrew spells this Quest would otherwise have had to write invented text for are gone from the Lodge now (Julio's call, 2026-07-15) — this Quest no longer owes any hand-written homebrew content. If either spell (or a new one) is reintroduced later, it enters as a fresh addition with its own text, not as unfinished business from here.
- Same purge, same day: the three *other* `(HB)` entries — `Freedom of the Waves (HB)`, `Widogast's Vault of Amber (HB)`, `Widogast's Web of Fire (HB)` (these had definitions, in `SPELL_DATA_2024` + 6 `spell_from_data` call sites) — were also removed. The verification pass should therefore expect **zero** `(HB)` markers anywhere in the Lodge; any that reappear are either deliberate new content or a regression.
- Worth deciding whether "verified" becomes a standing test (QST-0031.4) rather than a one-off check, given how easily 463 silent gaps accumulated the first time with nobody noticing until this was chased down by hand.
