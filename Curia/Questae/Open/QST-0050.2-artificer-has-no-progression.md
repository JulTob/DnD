## 🔍 Diagnosis (what & where)

`AtlasLusoris/Map_of_Classes/Codex_of_Progression.py`, `get_class_progression` (~line 48):

```python
    mapping = {
        "Barbarian": Barbarian,
        ...
        'Cleric':   Cleric,
        # Artificer?          <-- line 45, the gap, written as a question
        }
    prog_cls = mapping.get(class_name)
    return prog_cls(character)      # <-- line 48: None(character)
```

Two separate problems, and they compound:

1. **Artificer is absent from the mapping.** It is present in `GUILDS`, so the generator will happily choose it, and then has no progression to give it. The `# Artificer?` comment suggests this is a known, pre-existing gap rather than crisis damage — worth confirming against the tarball before treating it as a restore loss.
2. **The None-guard is in the wrong function.** `mapping.get` is written defensively, but its result is called immediately. The caller `GetFeatures` *does* guard (`if progression:`) — it just never gets the chance, because `get_class_progression` raises first.

## 🧾 Evidence

13 of 39 failures in a 180-Character sweep; **100% of Artificers**, from level 1 upward. First seen: Artificer, level 1, seed 7000.

```
TypeError: 'NoneType' object is not callable
  AtlasLusoris/Grimoire_of_Characters.py:251 in set_char_features
  Minion.py:267 in wrapped
  AtlasLusoris/Grimoire_of_Characters.py:405 in apply_class_features
  AtlasLusoris/Map_of_Classes/Codex_of_Progression.py:48 in get_class_progression
```

`grep -n "Artificer" Codex_of_Progression.py` returns exactly one line: the `# Artificer?` comment.

## 🎯 Desired outcome

Either an Artificer generates like any other Guild, or the generator does not offer a Guild it cannot build. Silently crashing on a Guild that appears in `GUILDS` is the one outcome to rule out.

## 🧭 Notes for the Agora / implementer

*Proposals, not decisions.*

- **These are two fixes and they are worth doing separately.**
  - *Guard (safe, immediate):* have `get_class_progression` return `None` when the Guild is unmapped, and let `GetFeatures`'s existing `if progression:` do its job. That converts a crash into a Character with no class features — visibly incomplete, but generable and inspectable.
  - *Progression (the real gap):* supply the Artificer progression class alongside its siblings. This is design work, not repair.
- **A question for the Agora before the second half:** is Artificer in scope for the beta at all? The recovery board records Julio's decision that the beta ships as a character generator only. If Artificer is out of scope, the honest fix is to **remove it from the selectable Guild list** rather than ship a Guild whose sheet is quietly empty — a missing Guild is a decision, an empty sheet is a bug report waiting to happen.
- **Do not** paper over this by mapping Artificer onto another class's progression.
- **Verify with:** `summon_player(guild="Artificer", level=1, seed=7000)` and the 180-Character sweep.
