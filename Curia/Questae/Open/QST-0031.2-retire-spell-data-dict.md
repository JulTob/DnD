## 🔍 Diagnosis (what & where)
`SPELL_DATA_2024` (`Lodge_of_Spells.py`, top of file) is a 139-entry dict — the *older* of the two parallel spell representations (see QST-0031's diagnosis). It's read today only through the `spell_from_data(name)` helper, which a handful of `Spell(...)` sites call instead of authoring their own definition inline. It is itself a source of truth competing with the `Spell` class it feeds.

## 🎯 Desired outcome
`SPELL_DATA_2024` is **deleted**, not deprecated-in-place. Every one of its 139 entries becomes a real `Spell(...)` instance (carrying its Tags per QST-0031.1) living directly in the Lodge's registry. `spell_from_data()` goes away with it — nothing left to route through.

## 🧭 Notes for the Agora / implementer
- Sequence **after** QST-0031.1 (Tags must exist to apply them during the migration — don't migrate first and tag later).
- This is a pure data migration (dict → `Spell()` calls) with no rules-text changes — the `definition` strings carry over verbatim from the dict. Low judgment-call risk, high mechanical volume.
- Double check every current `spell_from_data("X")` call site updates to reference the new canonical instance instead.
