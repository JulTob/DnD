## 🔍 Diagnosis (what & where)

Julio: *"Probably we should separate the Proficiency and Tools/Kits to a separate
module, and weapons, and armor, and other items… they all seem so different. Am I right
or too worried?"*

Right, and Canon agrees — but not uniformly across the four things named:

1. **`Grimoire_of_Items` was misnamed.** `Conventions.md` reserves `Grimoire_of_X` for
   "rites that invoke or coordinate entities" and warns it "is not permission to
   accumulate unrelated behavior." The module held a stable domain object (`Item`) plus
   its whole Tag family — which is the definition of a **Kit**: "the preferred home for
   a stable domain object and its related Tags."
2. **Proficiency was the genuinely mixed axis.** `GearKit` both decided *who may use
   what* (reading GuildKit training Tags) and *what a hero ends up with* (loadout
   policy). Two questions, one module.
3. **Tools were mixed into `Ledger_of_Gear`** alongside adventuring gear and packs. A
   tool is what a *proficiency* looks like — ability-governed, and now carrying an
   evocative `inspiration` line. Different axis from a bedroll.
4. **Weapons and Armor were already correctly separated** as their own Ledgers. No
   change needed — splitting further would have been the "too worried" half.

## 🧾 Evidence

`Conventions.md` on Kits: *"A Kit is the preferred home for a stable domain object and
its related Tags… One Tag family has one canonical Kit."*
`Modus-Operandi.md` design principles: *"One purpose per unit. Small Maps, Kits, and
components compose."* and *"Independent axes. Do not collapse semantically different
Tags into a convenient mixed category."*

## 🎯 Desired outcome

Each module answers one question, and its name says which.

## ✅ Resolution

- **Decided by:** Julio (asked the question; Canon supplied the boundary)
- **What changed:**
  - `Grimoire_of_Items.py` → **`ItemKit.py`** (all 8 importers repointed).
  - New **`Map_of_Gear_Proficiency.py`** — `armour_allowance`, `weapon_pool`,
    `may_use_shield`, `unarmoured_formula`, `has_unarmoured_defence`. A `Map` because it
    owns no Tag family; it ROUTES between GuildKit's training Tags and ItemKit's gear
    Tags. `GearKit` re-exports them so existing callers are unaffected.
  - New **`Ledger_of_Tools.py`** — 25 proficiency kits with prices, abilities, and
    inspirations. `Ledger_of_Gear` keeps adventuring gear and the seven packs.
  - Weapons and Armor Ledgers untouched — already one axis each.
- **Practice/preference to remember:** the naming convention is a real diagnostic, not
  decoration. "Is this a Grimoire or a Kit?" surfaced a design error that had been
  invisible while the code worked.
- **Moved to Solved:** 2026-08-01
