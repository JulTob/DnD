# Dialog 0015 — Bard: the core fantasy

- **Question:** What is the Bard's core fantasy, in what register should its guild prose be written, and how does each College refract it?
- **Commissioned by:** Julio (in chat, 2026-08-31), as part of the provisional class and subclass descriptions for the beta character generator.
- **Date:** 2026-08-31
- **Consuls called:** Lorekeeper (Elf Sage), Venustas (Bard), Workshop (Artificer), Vox to close.
- **Status:** 🟡 provisional texts below, pending Julio's word. Nothing here is a Decree.

---

## 🧭 Framing

The Fighter is done and its lesson is the method: find the one drive underneath the class ("training to beat the limits"), verify the 2024 kit actually delivers it, then write identity in second person with the inspiration in front and the rules nowhere. The Warlock patrons show the standard of evidence: every paragraph above `FIEND_DESCRIPTION` can point at the feature that underwrites it.

Constraints binding this Dialog:

- `Canon/Feature-Text.md`: explicit formatting, dice as notation, no open-choice language.
- House voice: second person, inspiration before rule, no em-dashes, Death of the Author (no proper-noun worldbuilding, no facts to memorize; things a player can inhabit).
- Backgrounds law generalized: no alignment-locking, no moral code baked into a class or College.
- Martial and technique fantasy draws Iberian and Eastern (Destreza, Sun Tzu, kendo, wuxia), not fechtbücher.
- One conflict of interest to declare: the Venustas seat is embodied as a Bard, and today the council deliberates the Bard. The seat argues from its lens, prose and register only, and the Lorekeeper holds the archetype. Recorded so nobody has to whisper it.

Julio gave no seed. Three candidates were put on the table by the commission itself: art as literal power over the world, the performer who is always on stage, and the collector of everything worth retelling. The council weighs all three.

---

## 🗣️ Deliberation

**Lorekeeper (Elf Sage):** I will lay out the three candidates with their bloodlines, because the Bard is the class with the oldest paper trail in the building and we should use it.

*Candidate A: art as literal power.* The deep well. Väinämöinen does not defeat Joukahainen with a sword; he sings him into the marsh, boots first (Kalevala, runo 3). Orpheus plays and stones move, beasts sit, and Death itself grants an exception to its one rule (Virgil, Georgics IV; Ovid, Metamorphoses X; still working in Monteverdi's L'Orfeo and again in Hadestown). Amergin claims a whole island by reciting what he is (Lebor Gabála Érenn). Taliesin's authority is that he was present at every event worth a poem. Tolkien opens creation itself as a choir (the Ainulindalë) and settles a duel between Finrod and Sauron as competitive singing, no swords drawn. Rothfuss's Kvothe hangs an entire saga on the gap between performing power and possessing it. And the 2024 rulebook itself joins this tradition: its own framing for bardic magic is that creation was spoken, and bards have caught fragments of the Words of Creation. This candidate is not our invention; it is the class's birth certificate.

*Candidate B: always on stage.* Scaramouche, born with the gift of laughter; Cyrano, who dies rather than surrender his panache; Figaro, who runs every scene he enters; Jaskier trailing the witcher to turn contracts into ballads. The rockstar fantasy in modern dress. It is charming and it is true of many bards at many tables.

*Candidate C: the collector.* Scheherazade, for whom the next story is literally survival (One Thousand and One Nights). The griot who is the archive of an empire (the Epic of Sundiata survives because they did). Demodocus, singing Odysseus's own war back to him until he weeps (Odyssey, book VIII). The seanchaí. The one who keeps everything worth retelling.

My claim: B and C are real, but they are *aspects*. B mistakes the stage presence for the power source, and C mistakes the library for what the library is for. A is the trunk; B and C are branches, and conveniently, the Colleges are waiting to be exactly those branches.

**Venustas Consul (Bard):** From my lens the three candidates sound different on the page, and that difference is evidence.

Candidate B writes itself into a corner. "You love an audience" is a personality, not a drive; it produces prose about the bard, preening, and the reader is left holding someone else's vanity. It also collapses at the campfire, in the dungeon, at the deathbed: the places the generated character will actually live. A fantasy that needs a stage is too small for a character sheet.

Candidate C writes beautifully but points backward. A collector's prose is all past tense: what you gathered, where you stood, what you saved. The sheet needs a present tense and a forward lean.

Candidate A is the only one where the prose can *enact its own claim*. If the class's thesis is that performance acts, then the description must itself be a performance: rhythm, repetition, a turn at the end. The reader should come away moved by a text about moving people. That is a register decision and I will make it formally below.

One refinement I insist on, though, before we crown A. Pure Orpheus is a soloist. Our Bard's spine, mechanically and emotionally, is not what the song does to stones; it is what it does to the friend standing next to you. I want the fantasy stated with the audience inside it. Not "art commands reality" but "what you perform becomes true in the people who hear it." Tyrtaeus is the cleaner ancestor here: Sparta did not keep his elegies because they were pretty, it kept them because lines that heard them held. And Lorca's duende lecture (Juego y teoría del duende) gives us the working definition of the register: art that risks something in the room, right now, or it is not art.

**Workshop Consul (Artificer):** Then let me test that refinement against the 2024 kit, because a fantasy the rules do not cash is a lie printed in second person. Rules read, from `AtlasLusoris/AtlasOfTraining/Map_of_Bard_Training.py` (real source, verified this session):

```
rules read — Bard core
  Bardic Inspiration (1)    a Bonus Action and a phrase, and an ally's failed
                            d20 becomes a success; the art acts, and it acts
                            THROUGH a hearer. The spine of the class.
  Spellcasting (1)          Charisma casts, through performance and wit; the
                            self is the instrument.
  Expertise (2)             two skills doubled; craft, not luck.
  Jack of All Trades (2)    half proficiency on everything; the one who picked
                            up a bit of every trade ever watched.
  Font of Inspiration (5)   inspiration back on a Short Rest; the well is
                            practice, not a potion.
  Countercharm (7)          your performance gives Advantage against Charmed
                            and Frightened to those who HEAR it; song contests
                            enchantment directly, Orpheus out-playing Sirens.
  Expertise II (9)          the craft widens.
  Magical Secrets (10)      two spells from ANY class list, always prepared;
                            the magpie: other people's magic is also worth
                            retelling.
  Superior Inspiration (18) roll Initiative empty, get one use back; the show
                            goes on.
  Words of Creation (20)    Power Word Heal and Power Word Kill, always
                            prepared, doubled to a second creature. No attack
                            roll. No save. At the capstone the metaphor drops:
                            the word alone does it.
```

Three findings, stated honestly rather than written around:

1. **The soloist version of Candidate A is cashed by RAW only at level 20.** Power Words are the one place a bard's utterance reshapes the world with no die in between. From 1 to 19, every signature effect routes through people: Bardic Inspiration is *given*, Countercharm protects *hearers*. So the Venustas refinement is not taste, it is the rules read back to us. The class text must center the audience and may only foreshadow the capstone.
2. **Hearing is load-bearing.** Bardic Inspiration wants the creature to see or hear you; Countercharm requires hearing at the start of the turn. The prose must claim power over *those who hear*, never over the world at large, or a silenced room falsifies the sheet.
3. **A gear-pipeline finding, for Julio rather than for prose.** Dance's opening feature, Dazzling Footwork (3), grants base AC 10 + Dexterity modifier + Charisma modifier *while wearing no armor and wielding no Shield*. But `unarmoured_formula` in `AtlasInventarium/GearKit.py` (line 194) knows only the Monk's Wisdom and the Barbarian's Constitution, and the stay-unarmoured logic keys on `Unarmed_Monk` / `Unarmed_Barb` proficiencies. The Bard guild trains Light armor, so the generator will dress a Dance Bard in leather and silently void the College's first feature. The fantasy is fine; the wardrobe department is not. This needs a Questa, not a rewrite.

With those recorded, I confirm the kit underwrites the refined Candidate A completely: the audience-shaped version, with the pure Orphic note held back as a level-20 promise.

**Lorekeeper (Elf Sage):** Accepting the refinement, and closing my own loop: it also settles where B and C live. The performer-always-on-stage is Glamour's inheritance; the collector is Lore's. The class fantasy has to be big enough that four Colleges can each take one facet and still be recognizably the same class. "What you perform becomes true in those who hear it" refracts four ways cleanly, as the subclass analyses below show. The trunk-and-branches structure holds.

**Venustas Consul (Bard):** Then the register, formally. Julio's rule is one register per class, chosen on purpose: the Ranger reads like an adventure novel, the Fighter like a training memoir. **The Bard reads like a piece performed aloud: incantatory spoken word.** Second person, present tense, short bars, anaphora, and one turn at the end of each text, because the class's whole claim is that saying is doing and the text must not be exempt from its own thesis. Duende as the quality bar: if a paragraph risks nothing, cut it. Constraints honored: no em-dashes (colons, parentheses, full stops carry the rhythm), no mechanics, no proper nouns, nothing the player must memorize, nothing alignment-locked. The creation-song is presented as hearsay ("they say"), per the Canon rule that the true mechanism is never explained on the page and never confirmed.

**Workshop Consul (Artificer):** No unanswered objection, provided finding 3 is carried to Julio as a finding and not smoothed over.

**Lorekeeper (Elf Sage):** No unanswered objection.

**Venustas Consul (Bard):** No unanswered objection, and the conflict of interest is noted a second time so the record is clean: the seat argued register only; the archetype was the Lorekeeper's to weigh, and the rules the Workshop's.

## 🎭 The Colleges, refracted

**Dance** — *The fight has a rhythm and you set it: your body is the instrument, and everyone in reach moves better because you lead.*

The candidate-B stage presence, moved into the body. The evidence is the tradition where fight and dance are one act: capoeira's roda, where nobody can say which of the two is happening; wuxia's balletic combat (Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon; the echo-drum game in House of Flying Daggers, a dance that is an ambush); flamenco's zapateado, rhythm as force of personality. Iberian and Eastern, per the standing ruling, and not a fencing manual in sight.

```
rules read — College of Dance
  Dazzling Footwork (3)   AC 10 + DEX + CHA, unarmored: grace and presence
                          literally deflect. Unarmed Strikes ride the Bardic
                          Inspiration die: the step IS the strike.
  Inspiring Movement (6)  an enemy closes, you move, and an ally moves with
                          you, nobody provokes: partner work, verbatim.
  Tandem Footwork (6)     one die, Initiative bonus for the whole ensemble:
                          you count them in.
  Leading Evasion (14)    your Evasion is shared with those beside you:
                          partners follow the lead out of the blast.
```

Every feature is choreography for more than one body. The College text therefore promises leading, not soloing. (The wardrobe finding from the deliberation applies here and is carried to Julio.)

**Glamour** — *Beauty as command: majesty borrowed from a stranger country, and the audience obeys a half-second before deciding to.*

Candidate B kept, but given teeth. The ancestry is the fae court, where beauty is sovereignty: Thomas the Rhymer taken by the Queen of Elfland, Tam Lin, Titania's retinue in A Midsummer Night's Dream. Tartt's line from The Secret History states the thesis: beauty is terror; what we call beautiful, we quiver before. The rules agree to an almost embarrassing degree: Unbreakable Majesty (14) makes an attacker save or *miss, recoiling from your majesty*; Mantle of Majesty (6) is Command as a Bonus Action, and a creature Charmed by you auto-fails; Beguiling Magic (3) hangs Charmed or Frightened off every Enchantment or Illusion; Mantle of Inspiration (3) lifts allies with Temporary Hit Points and free repositioning: the glamour extends over your own people too. This is genuine coercion in the rules, and per the backgrounds law the prose names the experience without moralizing it; a Glamour bard of any alignment reads true.

**Lore** — *The collector whose word can cut: everything worth knowing, kept, and aimed.*

Candidate C kept, with its edge showing. Scheherazade and the griot supply the keeping; the aiming comes from the satire tradition, where the word is a documented weapon: Cairbre's satire on Bres raises blotches and costs him a kingship (Cath Maige Tuired, the first satire of Ireland), and Archilochus's iambics were said to drive their targets to despair. Cutting Words (3) is that tradition as a Reaction: your die *subtracted* from their roll, criticism with a damage type. Bonus Proficiencies (3) widens the collection; Magical Discoveries (6) raids the archives of Cleric, Druid and Wizard; Peerless Skill (14) finally spends the hoard on yourself, and a failure does not even consume the die: the collection is that deep.

**Valor** — *The war-poet: the song carried to where songs are tested, chorus and blade in the same breath.*

The class fantasy at its most literal. Tyrtaeus, whose elegies Sparta kept as equipment; Thormod reciting the Bjarkamál to the army before Stiklestad (Heimskringla); Taillefer at Hastings, singing of Roland ahead of the first charge; the juglar of the Cantar de Mio Cid, carrying deeds back out again so they happened twice. For the blade itself, the Eastern half of the ruling: kendo's kiai, the shout that is part of the strike, breath and blow one event. The rules deliver exactly this: Combat Inspiration (3) puts your die onto an ally's AC or damage (the verse lands on shield and on blade); Martial Training (3) makes the weapon itself your spellcasting focus (the sword IS the instrument, verbatim RAW); Extra Attack (6) lets a cantrip replace a swing (verse and cut interchangeable); Battle Magic (14) is the spell and the strike in one breath.

## 📜 The proposed texts

Ready to lift verbatim. Plain prose, paragraphs separated by blank lines; they become Python string constants when the kits are wired.

**CLASS_DESCRIPTION (Bard)**

```
They say the world began as a song. Every people tells it differently: a word in the dark, a drum, a wheel of stars keeping time. Nobody remembers the tune. But you have caught pieces of it. You hear them where nobody else is listening: in a tavern chorus, in a mourner's wail, in the held breath before a crowd decides.

You learned early what a performance does. Not what it says: what it does. A room of strangers breathes together. A tired line stands back up. A tyrant hears one verse about himself and never sleeps the same again. Others dig their power out of libraries, bloodlines, bargains. Yours happened in front of everyone, and they applauded.

So you play, and things come true. Courage comes true. The right word on the right beat, and a friend becomes, for one moment, exactly who they needed to be. You are not describing the world. You are conducting it. And on the good nights, under everything, you can almost hear the first song still playing, waiting for someone with the nerve to sing along.
```

**DANCE_DESCRIPTION**

```
Your instrument is the one you were born holding. You tuned it the way every musician tunes: one step, ten thousand mornings, until balance stopped being effort and became opinion.

Now every fight is a piece you choreograph from inside it. A quarter turn, and the blow meant for you applauds empty air. You catch an ally's eye like taking a hand, and the whole floor moves where you lead. The others say battle is chaos. You know better. It keeps a rhythm. You set it.
```

**GLAMOUR_DESCRIPTION**

```
Some beauty comforts. Yours arrives. You spent a while in a country whose light does not behave (or that light spent a while in you), and you came back wearing it. Rooms go quiet a half beat after you enter, and nobody can say why they stood.

You know what majesty actually is. It is not being loved. It is being answered before anyone decides to answer. A crown is a rumor of it; you are the fact, one held breath at a time. And a held breath, held well, is enough.
```

**LORE_DESCRIPTION**

```
You collect. Songs, sciences, scandals, recipes, prayers, the true reason the war started. Anything one person thought worth saying once, you think worth keeping forever. You have stood in the rain to hear the end of a story.

None of it is trivia. That is what they miss. Knowledge has an edge, and you keep yours sharp and close at hand. The right fact at the right moment opens a gate, or a grave. The right word, placed exactly, takes the strength out of a giant's swing. They laughed at the little library walking behind the swords. They stopped.
```

**VALOR_DESCRIPTION**

```
Anyone can sing about courage in a warm hall. You carry the song where songs are tested: into the cold, into the line, shield up, where every verse can be checked against what you do next.

This is the whole art. The beat that keeps a line breathing. The note under a friend's swing that sends it home. Your own blade, arriving on the downbeat. Heroes are made twice, once in the field and once in the telling, and you are present for both. You make sure of both.
```

## 🕊️ Vox report

**The choice made.** The council converged on an audience-shaped version of the Orphic fantasy: *what you perform becomes true in the people who hear it.* Not the soloist who commands stones (the rules cash that only at level 20, and the council refused to promise at level 1 what RAW delivers at 20), and not the mere charmer or the mere archivist (those are Colleges, and they got them: Glamour inherits the stage, Lore inherits the collection). The spine is Bardic Inspiration read literally: a phrase, spoken on a beat, turns a friend's failure into success. Register: incantatory spoken word, second person, present tense, one turn per text, because a class whose thesis is that saying is doing cannot have a description exempt from its own claim.

**The strongest rival.** Pure Candidate A, the soloist Orpheus: art as direct power over the world, no audience required. It has the deepest literary bloodline and the level-20 capstone genuinely delivers it (Power Word Heal and Power Word Kill: no roll, no save). The council set it aside because nineteen levels of the kit route every signature effect through a hearer, and a description must be true of the character at every level it ships at. It survives inside the chosen text as the foreshadowed "first song underneath."

**Open questions for Julio.**

1. **The Dance wardrobe.** Dazzling Footwork (AC 10 + DEX + CHA, unarmored, no Shield) is invisible to `unarmoured_formula` and to the stay-unarmoured logic in `AtlasInventarium/GearKit.py`; a generated Dance Bard will be dressed in Light armor and lose the College's opening feature. Needs a Questa; the prose promises grace-as-armor on the strength of RAW, so the pipeline should catch up rather than the text backing down.
2. **The creation-song as hearsay.** The class text opens "They say the world began as a song." This deliberately mirrors the Canon rule that the true mechanism is never confirmed on the page. If Julio would rather the Bard not touch cosmogony at all, the first paragraph can be rebuilt around the tavern-and-mourner listening imagery alone; the rest stands either way.
3. **Glamour's coercion.** The rules make beauty genuinely coercive (auto-failed saves under Mantle of Majesty). The proposed text names the experience without moralizing it, per the backgrounds law. If Julio wants the College to read warmer or colder, that is a one-paragraph dial, and the council took the neutral setting on purpose.
