About Us

Our Mission

Gen Legend was created to make character creation for tabletop RPGs and storytelling easy, fun, and accessible. Especially for newcomers! We love Dungeons & Dragons, but also believe everyone should be able to quickly generate engaging, memorable characters without being overwhelmed by complex rules or lore. This tool aims to help the newcomers, and inspire the Old Guard of D&D. Either for a Dungeon Crawler, or the Adventure of a Lifetime, we made Gen Legend to help you.

How the Generator Works

Our generator crafts unique characters and NPCs at the click of a button. It draws from thoughtfully curated lists of names, quirks, fantasy roles, and more. Each time you generate, it combines random elements guided by creative logic to produce characters that are interesting, plausible, and ready to drop into your campaign or story.

  • Noob-friendly:

    You don’t need to be an expert! The interface is simple and guides you through generation with clear options.

  • Instant variety:

    Looking for a villain, quirky shopkeeper, or heroic ally? The generator can help you in seconds. We built the NPC generator with Dungeon Masters in mind!

  • Creative:

    Generated characters often include quirks or hooks to inspire unique scenes and roleplay.

Why Gen Legend?

We know that planning a story or RPG session can be time consuming, and sometimes inspiration runs dry, or you find yourself improvising an unexpected scene. Our goal is to help both newcomers and experienced storytellers fill that gap, whether you need a sudden NPC or want to flesh out a protagonist for your next big adventure.

Jump in, start creating, and let your imagination take the lead!
