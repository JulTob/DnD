## The class fantasy

A Cleric is someone **kept**. Something watches *over* them — a god, ancestors,
the universe, luck, "the Force." The identity stays a guess so the generator
and the player can fill it. Recognition (being seen) is the ego-boost and the
burden. Percy Jackson / chosen / prophesied is in-scope as *favorite of the
watcher*, not as a second core.

Invite the watching. Do not name it as surveillance.

## Register

Biblical / Jedi cadence as a **tool**: parallelism, parataxis, concrete images.
Not Christian theming. Not archaic costume (`thee`, `shall`).
Dante, Greek god-texts, Nietzsche's religious voice are fair inspirations.

**Vigilant verbs frighten; committed verbs comfort.**
"Watches," "does not sleep," "does not look away" read as inspection on a
character sheet. Warm scripture renders care as shelter, carrying, remembering,
and answering before you ask.

## Domain as doctrine, not care-style caption

| Trickery | facilitating / spoiling parent | the watcher cheats so you get away with things; gaze does not look away |
| War | military father; the gaze *demands* | stand; the bar does not move |
| Grave | the gaze *respects* the line | what should rest, rest; sometimes bend the line back |
| Knowledge | (session: work the lesson until it is yours) | teach yourself on behalf of what taught you first |
