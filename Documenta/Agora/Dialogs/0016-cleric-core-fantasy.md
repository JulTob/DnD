## 🧭 Framing

This Dialog has a different shape from its siblings. For the Bard the council derived a fantasy from nothing; here Julio has already given it, twice over. The seed, binding: **the core fantasy of the Cleric is to be seen, watched over, by a parental entity** (a god, usually), and **the Domains are parenting styles** that determine religious principle and behavior (nurturing, demanding, playful, disciplinarian). And the class voice already exists: the warm guild block Julio accepted at 02:31 on 2026-08-31 ships in `Map_of_Cleric_Prayers.py` as `CLERIC_DESCRIPTION`, seated by `bind_cleric_voice` (commit "Give the Cleric a voice: the watcher fantasy, warm register").

So the council's work is threefold: **verify** the seed against the 2024 rules honestly (if the rules had fought it, that would be a finding, not something to write around); **document** the found voice so the register survives the recovery; and **write the four Domain openings** the generator's roster needs (Life, Light, Trickery, War), which QST-0080.1 records as still in the plainer pass. The salvaged class text is not replaced. The shipping `DOMAIN_FRAMES` (which seat the drawn prayer) are not replaced either; the openings must end where their frame can follow.

Constraints from Canon: `Feature-Text.md` (identity, never a mechanics summary; explicit breaks; no open-choice language), no em-dashes in authored prose, Death of the Author (no lore-dumps, no named theology; the watcher stays a guess), traits taught rather than inherited, and the standing ruling that martial and technique fantasy draws on Iberian and Eastern traditions. From the recovery vault, two register laws Julio already ratified: biblical/Jedi cadence is a **tool** (parallelism, parataxis, concrete images), never archaic costume; and **vigilant verbs frighten, committed verbs comfort**: "watches, does not sleep, does not look away" read as inspection on a sheet, while warm scripture renders care as shelter, carrying, and answering before you ask.

## 🗣️ Deliberation

**Lorekeeper (Elf Sage):** The seed is binding, so let me do what a binding seed still requires: place it in the tradition, and set two rivals against it so we know it wins on evidence rather than on decree. Julio's fantasy is the oldest register religious writing has. The 121st Psalm is nothing but this: the keeper who keeps you, going out and coming in. The 23rd renders it as shepherding, which is parenthood with a crook. Dante closes the entire *Commedia* on it: the love that moves the sun and the other stars, the universe itself as a face turned toward you. And notice that the shipping guild block already knows this: "Maybe the universe itself, turning its face toward you" is Dante's ending in the project's voice. The modern carriers are just as clear. Obi-Wan's "the Force will be with you, always" is a dead mentor still watching, and it comforts rather than frightens. Percy Jackson is the whole seed in a franchise: a child of a god, waiting to be *claimed*, and the claiming is at once the ego-boost and the burden. The vault records Julio keeping that in scope as *favorite of the watcher*, not as a second core. Tevye in *Fiddler on the Roof* gives us the register's conversational floor: a man who talks to God the way you talk to a father who can be argued with. And Marilynne Robinson's *Gilead* is the purest prose model we have: an old father writing to a young son, every page a hand held out in advance.

**Venustas (Bard):** Name the rivals, then, because I can feel both of them pulling at the class.

**Lorekeeper (Elf Sage):** Rival one, call it **the conduit**: the mortal through whom the power passes. Moses with his arms up over the sea, Joan of Arc and her voices, Constantine's sign in the sky, and the game's own folk memory of the class: Van Helsing brandishing the cross, the priest in every vampire story whose faith is a floodlight. Rival two, **the intercessor**: the one who stands between a community and heaven. Bernanos' country priest, Father Zosima in *The Brothers Karamazov*, Bishop Myriel in *Les Misérables*, whose one act of mercy redirects an entire novel.

**Venustas (Bard):** I will argue both rivals properly before we bury them. The conduit is a real fantasy players arrive with: the moment the dark thing recoils, the hand that glows, the body as lightning rod. It is also, mechanically, what a Cleric does every round of play, and a description that ignores the class's daily verbs is perfume. The intercessor is real too: the shepherd who answers for the flock is the most human priest fiction has, and mercy-as-power (Myriel's candlesticks) is genuinely moving material.

**Contracts (Warlock):** And both fail on my desk before they fail on yours. The conduit describes the *mechanism* and not the *motive*: it says what passes through you and never why you walk into the crypt. Worse, it collapses the Cleric into my own seat's class. A Warlock is also a mortal channel for a greater being's power; if the Cleric's fantasy is "borrowed lightning," the two classes are one class with different bookkeeping. The project already draws the true line, and it drew it in the Fiend text: "Something on the other side of it noticed." The Fiend's watcher *notices*, the way an opportunist notices an unlocked window. The salvaged Cleric block answers it, sentence for sentence: "In the hour you falter, a hand is held out. It was held out before you reached for it." Noticing is conditional. Keeping is prior. **The Warlock is seen as useful; the Cleric is seen as beloved.** That distinction is the class boundary, and it is Julio's seed stated as a contract.

**Simplicity (Monk):** The intercessor fails simpler than that. It faces the congregation, and the generator's Cleric faces the road. And it quietly class-locks priesthood: the project's own canon says a background (Acolyte, say) must never be moral-coded or welded to a class, and the intercessor fantasy would weld the whole profession of faith to this one Guild. The seed keeps faith personal and portable: not "you hold an office" but "you are held." Anyone can be held. That is why it generates.

**Lorekeeper (Elf Sage):** Agreed, and one more note for the record before the rules read: the seed also survives the project's hardest canon test, *taught, not inherited*. Being watched over is not a bloodline and not a birthright; it is a relationship, and every Domain below renders it as something *shown to you* that you now practice. The texts should say "the way you were shown" and mean it.

**Venustas (Bard):** Then the register, which in this Dialog is documentation rather than invention. The found voice is **warm scripture**: second person, parallel clauses, short declaratives, the miracle kept domestic ("you have been carried farther than you know"). Its three devices are worth naming so nobody sands them off later. First, the triple "Maybe": the watcher's identity is offered as a guess three times and never resolved, which is Death of the Author performed in-text; the player fills the mold. Second, the pre-emptive hand: "It was held out before you reached for it" makes the care *prior*, which is the entire difference from the Warlock. Third, the turn in the last line: "contemplated miracles long enough you started doing your own" pivots the whole class from receiving to giving. Hold that pivot; it is about to organize the Domains.

**Simplicity (Monk):** Say the organizing rule in one line before anyone writes four paragraphs.

**Venustas (Bard):** *Being watched over teaches you to watch over.* Each Domain is a parenting style received, and each Domain's doctrine is that same style turned outward. The child grows into the parent.

**Contracts (Warlock):** And I can verify that line mechanically, which surprised me. In every one of the four Domains, the level-3-and-up kit hands the received care onward. Life: your healing spells heal *others* more, and healing others heals *you*. Light: Warding Flare spoils an attack on *anyone you can see*, the lamp lifted for another walker. Trickery: Blessing of the Trickster is literally transitive, you pass Advantage to a companion. War: Guided Strike corrects a missed attack, *yours or an ally's*. Four Domains, one shape: what was done for you, you do for others. The rules were read for the thesis and the rules agree with it.

**Simplicity (Monk):** Then my disciplines for the texts. One: the openings complete a machine that already runs. `voice_of_domain` renders opening, blank line, then the Domain's prayer frame ("In a low hour, a line came back to you...") around a drawn prayer. So each opening must end on ground the frame can stand on, and must not pre-empt the ledger: no locked doors in Trickery's opening while "Not every closed door is locked" sits in its prayer pool, no dawn aphorisms in Light's while "The dark is patient. So is the dawn." waits to be drawn. I will check every draft against its pool. Two: doctrine, not caption. QST-0080.1 records Julio rejecting care-style summaries as loredump; each opening must say how you *behave*, in second person, and let the style be inferred. Three: the vigilant-verb law holds hardest in Trickery and War, where the styles flirt with surveillance and drill-sergeant cruelty. Demand must read as care or the seed dies on the page.

**Lorekeeper (Elf Sage):** One caution from the sources before we refract. The seed's failure mode is saccharine: an unbroken warmth that costs nothing and therefore moves nobody. Scripture never makes that mistake; the kept child still walks the valley of the shadow. The Domain texts should each keep one hard edge (triage, scorch, the unmoving bar, the theft you did commit) so the warmth has something to be warm against.

## 🎭 The Domains: four parenting styles

Each Domain bends the class fantasy (*someone keeps you; being watched over teaches you to watch over*) through a different style of care, and the rules were read for every claim.

### Life : the nurturing parent

**One line:** *Falling is not the end; you were picked up without being asked to earn it, and now you are the one who stays.*

The caring mother, mercy without arithmetic. The founding image is the Prodigal Son: the father runs to meet the returning child and never once counts the debt, which is why Julio's accepted opening says "You do not count whether you earned the rising." Behind it, Demeter, who will stop the world's seasons for one daughter; Marmee in *Little Women*, teaching mercy by doing it in front of you; and the ledger's own Japanese proverb, fall seven times, stand up eight. Rules read: Disciple of Life (every healing spell heals 2 + spell level more: care compounds), Preserve Life (five times your level in Hit Points, distributed among the Bloodied, nobody past half: triage, the mother's arithmetic, most to the worst hurt), Blessed Healer (healing others heals you: mercy feeds the merciful, the thesis as a level 6 feature), Supreme Healing (every die maxes: mercy without a stint), and a spell list that runs Cure Wounds to Revivify to Heal. "Falling is not the end" is Revivify stated as doctrine. There is no damage feature anywhere in the Domain: the kit is care all the way up. Julio's opening already carries this register; the council touches one character of it (an em-dash, mended per the standing rule) and otherwise leaves it as found.

### Light : the teacher who shows the road

**One line:** *You were shown the road, never carried down it; now you lift the lamp for whoever walks beside you.*

The teacher who shows and then lets you choose, Julio's sketch verbatim: "next step appears; you set the direction." The founding images are guides who illuminate and release: Virgil, who leads Dante as far as his light reaches and then must let him go on; Plato's cave, where the whole education is being turned toward the light and the walking out is your own; Yoda and Obi-Wan, who show and refuse to do; Amaterasu drawn out of the cave, light returning to a world that had learned what its absence costs. Rules read: the Domain grants *sight* before it grants fire (See Invisibility, Arcane Eye, Scrying on the spell list: the watcher teaches watching), Warding Flare (a Reaction that spoils an attack on anyone you can see: the lamp lifted for another walker), Improved Warding Flare (the flare now also shelters its target with Temporary Hit Points: the teacher's hand steadies as it deflects), Radiance of the Dawn (Magical Darkness within 30 feet is dispelled, no save, then the searing: the road laid bare, whatever was hiding on it), Corona of Light (you emanate sunlight: the pupil becomes the lamp, the thesis as a capstone). One honest tension: Burning Hands, Fireball, Wall of Fire, Flame Strike. The teacher who shows also scorches, and the text says so rather than writing around it.

### Trickery : the accomplice parent

**One line:** *You got away with too much for luck to be the whole story; what was covered for you, you now cover for others.*

The facilitating, spoiling parent: the watcher who cheats so you get away with things, whose gaze does not look away and does not tell. The founding image is the Homeric Hymn to Hermes: the infant steals Apollo's cattle, lies about it beautifully, and Zeus *laughs*, the divine parent charmed by the theft. Beside it, Yashoda catching Krishna with the stolen butter and finding the universe in his mouth: the indulged child who was always more than the mischief. And the fairy godmother, who breaks the rules of the world on your behalf and sets a curfew of midnight. Rules read: Blessing of the Trickster (transitive by design: you hand Advantage on Stealth to a willing companion: the wink passed on, the thesis at level 3), Invoke Duplicity (a perfect double of you, placed where you are not: an alibi authored by heaven, and Advantage when it distracts), Trickster's Transposition (swap places with the double: you were never where trouble looked), Improved Duplicity (when the illusion ends it *heals* someone: the joke was a kindness all along), and a spell list that is one long getting-away-with-it: Pass Without Trace, Invisibility, Nondetection, Dimension Door. One flag for Julio rather than a workaround: Charm Person, Dominate Person and Modify Memory sit at the cold end of this kit, and the playful-parent frame stretches thinnest at Dominate Person. The text promises complicity and cover; it does not promise the domination is kind.

### War : the demanding parent

**One line:** *The demand was the care all along; the bar does not move, and now you hold others to the line the way you were held.*

The military father, the gaze that demands: "stand; the bar does not move." Per the standing ruling the sources are Iberian and Eastern, and they are rich here. La Verdadera Destreza built swordsmanship as geometry and self-command: the diestro's first conquered enemy is his own temper, and the drill is love expressed as exactness. The kendo dojo says the same: the sensei's severity *is* the care, and nobody drills a student they have given up on. Sun Tzu supplies the doctrine's cold clarity (the ledger already carries "Never interrupt an enemy making a mistake"), and the stoic line "the person who masters himself is ultimately undefeatable" sits in the War prayer pool as its creed. One scriptural image for the register rather than the page: the centurion of Capernaum, who frames absolute faith in chain-of-command grammar, and is marveled at for it. Rules read: Guided Strike (a Reaction granting +10 to a *missed* attack roll, yours or an ally's: correction arriving exactly at the moment of failure, the parenting style as a game mechanic, and the thesis too, since you correct allies' swings the way yours were corrected), War Priest (extra strikes rationed by your Wisdom: attention is the resource being spent), War God's Blessing (Shield of Faith or Spiritual Weapon, free and without Concentration: the parent takes the field beside you), Avatar of Battle (Resistance to weapon damage: what the drill was for: you can take the hit and stand), and a spell list whose first entry is literally Guiding Bolt, with Steel Wind Strike at the top for the eastern blade the ruling asks for.

**CLASS_DESCRIPTION** (as found in `Map_of_Cleric_Prayers.py`, accepted by Julio 2026-08-31 02:31; ships today)

```
There is something watching over you, and it has been with you always. Maybe a god. Maybe your ancestors. Maybe the universe itself, turning its face toward you. Whatever it is, you have been carried farther than you know.

You can feel it. Maybe you don't see it, and you may not be able to explain why, but still you do believe. You believe you are meant for something. What it is, you are not sure you want to know — and you will not be asked for more than you can carry. You only hope you will be enough. In the hour you falter, a hand is held out. It was held out before you reached for it.

You have defied the odds, and contemplated miracles long enough you started doing your own.

**LIFE_DESCRIPTION** (Julio's accepted opening, as found, with the em-dash mended to a comma; no other change)

```
Falling is not the end, while there is still breath to return to. Until you can stand again.

You do not count whether you earned the rising. You learn to stay. To restore. To heal, the way you were shown.

**LIGHT_DESCRIPTION**

```
You were not carried over the dark ground. You were shown it. A lamp lifted at the right turn, the road laid bare to the next rise, and the walking left to you.

That is the whole teaching, and you keep it now. Light does not choose for anyone. It shows the fair and the foul in their true colors, and trusts the walker with the difference. So you lift the lamp higher than is comfortable, for whoever shares the road.

Some of what you show warms. Some of it scorches. The lamp does not flinch, and neither do you.
```

**TRICKERY_DESCRIPTION**

```
Some children are watched the way a hawk watches. You were watched the way an accomplice watches: warmly, closely, with a straight face kept for the guards.

You got away with too much, too often, for luck to be the whole story. The sentry who yawned at the right moment. The count that came out right on the wrong night. The fall that found the hay cart. Behind it all, a smile that has never once looked away, and never once told.

So that is your doctrine, the only one you keep: the wink passed on. What was covered for you, you cover for others.
```

**WAR_DESCRIPTION**

```
Nothing you brought back was ever quite enough. You hated that, until you understood: the demand was the care. Nobody drills a child they have given up on.

So you learn to stand when lying down would be easier. To stand where you said you would stand. To master the hand, then the temper, then the fear, because the enemy is whatever is still unmastered. And when your blow goes wide, the correction comes, firm as a hand over your hand on the grip.

The bar does not move. That is how you know it was set with love.
```

## 🕊️ Vox report

**The choice made.** Julio's seed was binding and the council confirms it is *right*, not merely ordered: the Cleric's core fantasy is **to be kept, watched over by a parental entity**, and the 2024 rules establish it at both ends of the class. Spellcasting is "prayer and meditation" (a conversation, heard) at level 1; Divine Intervention *guarantees* an answer daily at level 10, where the 2014 rules made it a failing gamble, meaning the official redesign moved toward exactly Julio's fantasy; and the capstone is Wish: the parent will give you anything. The class boundary against the Warlock is drawn as a contract: the Warlock's patron *notices* (conditional, transactional: "Something on the other side of it noticed"); the Cleric's watcher *keeps* (prior, unconditional: "It was held out before you reached for it"). The shipping class voice was documented as found (warm scripture: parallelism, parataxis, concrete images, the triple "Maybe" that leaves the watcher a guess) and is untouched. The organizing thesis for the Domains, verified feature by feature: **being watched over teaches you to watch over**; every Domain's kit hands the received care onward (Blessed Healer, Warding Flare, Blessing of the Trickster, Guided Strike). Domain fantasies as parenting styles, per the seed: Life, the nurturing parent (mercy without arithmetic; Julio's own opening kept, one character mended); Light, the teacher who shows the road and lets you choose; Trickery, the accomplice parent whose gaze never looks away and never tells; War, the demanding parent whose unmoving bar is the proof of love, on Destreza, kendo and Sun Tzu per the standing Iberian-and-Eastern ruling. Tone modulation per Domain: lullaby, lesson, conspiracy, drill. All four openings are written to seat the existing prayer frames, and were checked against their prayer pools for image collisions.

**The strongest rival.** The **conduit** (the mortal channel of borrowed lightning: Moses, Joan of Arc, the game's own Van-Helsing folk memory of the class). It is what a Cleric mechanically does every round, and Channel Divinity is nearly its name. It lost because it states the mechanism and never the motive, and because it collapses the Cleric into the Warlock's shape, erasing the beloved/useful line that both classes' texts now hold from opposite sides. The intercessor (the shepherd who answers for a flock) fell faster: it faces the congregation rather than the road, and it would class-lock priesthood in a project whose canon keeps backgrounds and vocations free.

**Open questions for Julio.**
1. The shipping class block carries one em-dash ("want to know — and you will not be asked"). The standing rule forbids them; the council did not edit the accepted text. Mend it (comma, colon, or full stop), or bless it as the exception?
2. Life's opening is Julio's own accepted text with the em-dash mended to a comma ("To heal, the way you were shown."). Bless the mend, or restore the original punctuation?
3. Trickery's spell list ends at Dominate Person and Modify Memory, and the playful-parent frame stretches thinnest there. The proposed text promises complicity and cover, not that the domination is kind. Is that the right amount of shadow, or should the text acknowledge the cold end explicitly?
4. Trickery's first sentence names the cold gaze ("watched the way a hawk watches") in order to refuse it. The council reads this as inside the letter of "never name the watcher as an inspector," because the sentence performs the vigilant-verbs-frighten rule and then chooses warmth; flagged in case Julio reads it otherwise.
5. Grave and Knowledge also sit in `DOMAIN_OPENINGS` in the plainer register, but they are outside the generator's roster. Raise them now for symmetry under QST-0080.1, or leave them to that Questa's own pass?
