## 🔍 Diagnosis (what & where)

Recorded after the Grok loss, because this design existed only as code and the
code went behind a bytecode shim. `Documenta/Canon/Cultural-Inspirations.md`
holds the lore; this questa holds the *engineering* decisions behind it.

**1. Atomic societies, no invented middle grounds.** The first pass used blended
keys (`japonic_aztec`, `norse_steppe_celtic`, `alpine_renaissance`,
`indo_persian_levantine`, `sinic_indic`). Julio: *"don't invent 'middle lands'
for cultures, just 'levante' or 'eastern islands', not a made up middleground or
mix… a fantasy species may have more than one (aztec+japan)."* So every marker is
now a real culture named for itself, and a species that sits between traditions
simply **holds both markers**.

**2. Elementals were missing entirely.** Julio: *"India was for the elementals,
along with oceania (water elementals). I completely forgot."* Recorded with the
caste-tension society and mixed elementals as outcasts, even though no elemental
is playable in 2024, so it cannot be forgotten a second time.

**3. Legend markers beside the real ones.** Julio: *"you may also add variations
like 'fairytale_elf' or 'folklore_dwarf' … even 'Tolkien_Elfs' and
'Eragon_Dragons' even 'anime' for monks."* Twelve legend markers feed the **same**
reach map as the societies, so a name is one roll across both families. They live
in a separate `_LEGEND_NOUNS` table because a wrong people is a history question
and a wrong legend is a genre question: different edits, different reviewers.

**4. The double mapping.** Julio: *"For errors like this is useful to have a
species - society - weapon double mapping. Would make these errors an easy fix."*
`societies_of()` and `legends_of()` split what `cultures_of()` merges, so the
Aasimar/Goliath mix-up in QST-0046.4 was a one-line diagnosis.

**5. A shared budget, not a weight per marker.** Multi-marker species were
shouting louder than single-marker ones: a Human (4 societies) paid four times
the cultural weight, drowned the trade layer, and broke the rustic-Spear test.
Fixed with `_CULTURAL_BUDGET = 12` **split across** the markers held, plus
`_INFLUENCE_SCALE = 3` and rescaled `_GENERIC_WEIGHT = 4` / `_TRADE_WEIGHT = 16`
so a one-marker species reproduces the old balance exactly.

  *Wyrmrider's Greatblade* from one roll.

## 🎯 Desired outcome

The design survives independently of the code. When QST-0093.2 lands the source,
these five decisions are checkable against it rather than remembered.

## 🧭 Notes for the Agora / implementer

- The budget split is the subtle one. Removing it does not fail loudly; it makes
  rich-heritage species quietly monotonous. The rustic-Spear share assertion is
  what catches it.
- Culture-marked words must never sit in the generic pool. That leak is how a
  Dwarf once carried a *Yari*, and it recurred once after compounding.
