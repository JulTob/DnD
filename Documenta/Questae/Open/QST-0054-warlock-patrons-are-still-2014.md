# QST-0054 — Warlock patron features are a 2014/2024 mixture

- **Type:** rule-update
- **Priority:** 🔴 urgent
- **Status:** Open
- **Related:** `AtlasLusoris/AtlasOfTraining/Map_of_Warlock_Training.py`

---

## 🔍 Diagnosis

The four patron **spell lists** were brought to 2024. The patron **features**
were not, and several are the 2014 text wearing a 2024 name.

**Great Old One** — the worst affected.

- *Psychic Spells* was missing the entire damage-type clause, its main
  benefit, and carried an invented clause about casting with Pact slots
  (meaningless, since every Warlock slot is a Pact slot). **Fixed.**
- *Awakened Mind* is the 2014 version: at-will 30-foot telepathy. 2024 is a
  Bonus Action, range in **miles** equal to Charisma modifier, duration in
  minutes equal to Warlock level, requires a shared language, and ends if you
  connect to someone else.
- *Eldritch Hex* is invented. 2024: you always have **Hex** prepared, and when
  you cast it the target also has Disadvantage on saves of the chosen ability.
- *Create Thrall* is 2014 (touch an Incapacitated humanoid to Charm it). 2024
  modifies **Summon Aberration** to drop Concentration, gives the Aberration
  Temporary Hit Points, and adds Hex damage on its first hit each turn.

**Archfey**

- *Misty Escape* is 2014 (turn Invisible and teleport 60 feet). 2024 is "cast
  Misty Step as a Reaction when you take damage", plus two new Steps of the Fey
  options: **Disappearing Step** and **Dreadful Step**.
- *Beguiling Defenses* has the 2014 charm-reflection. 2024 is Charmed immunity
  plus a Reaction that halves damage from an attacker and forces a Wisdom save
  for Psychic damage equal to what you took.
- *Bewitching Magic* casts Misty Step **as part of the same action**, not as a
  Bonus Action teleport.

**Fiend**

- *Dark One's Own Luck* is 2014. 2024 gives uses equal to Charisma modifier,
  regained on a Long Rest, usable **after seeing the roll**, once per roll.
- *Fiendish Resilience* still carries the 2014 "magical or silvered weapons
  ignore this" clause. 2024 drops it and excludes **Force** from the choice.
- *Hurl Through Hell* is missing the Charisma save, the 8d10 Psychic, the
  Incapacitated condition, and the Pact-slot restore.

**Celestial**

- *Searing Vengeance* is self-only. 2024 triggers when **you or an ally within
  60 feet** is about to make a Death Saving Throw, and adds 2d8 + Charisma
  Radiant damage and the Blinded condition around the saved creature.

## 🎯 Desired outcome

All 22 patron features match the 2024 text, in the passive voice settled in
QST-0055, with numbers resolved where the sheet knows them.
