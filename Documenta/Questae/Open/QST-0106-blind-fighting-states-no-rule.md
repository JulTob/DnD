## 🔍 Diagnosis (what & where)

Blind Fighting is the only Fighting Style whose entry states no rule. Its
description is *"You trained to fight without sight."* and a Blindsight chip of
10 feet. The eleven siblings each print their rule.

The chip-only shape is the house senses pattern used elsewhere, but here it is
applied where every sibling prints the mechanic, so the reader gets a range with
no statement of what it does.

## 🎯 Desired outcome

1. The entry states the rule: you have Blindsight with a range of 10 feet,
   within which you can see anything that is not behind Total Cover even if you
   are Blinded or in Darkness, and you automatically detect an invisible
   creature there unless it successfully hides from you.
2. The chip stays, since a chip is a lookup and the prose is the entry.
