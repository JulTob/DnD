## 🔍 Diagnosis (what & where)

`AtlasLusoris/Grimoire_of_Guilds.py` is a `marshal.loads` shim over `.recovery-vault/GuildKit.cpython-314.pyc`. Every Player build executes it. Its bytecode was compiled against the old TagKit API (`Report(value)`), so it cannot be migrated; and it cannot be read.

The authentic source survives in history: commit `65edc0f` ("GuildKit returns as its authentic pre-wipe source"), 2,997 lines, no `marshal`. Compared name for name with the live bytecode module's `__all__` on 2026-09-06: all 100 public names present, nothing missing. It carries one `Report(value)` site (line 1632) and one `Has`.

## 🧾 Evidence

- `git show 65edc0f:AtlasLusoris/GuildKit.py | wc -l` → 2997; AST top-level names ⊇ live `__all__` (100 of 100).
- Live shim header: "keeps the surviving Aug 28 bytecode for Build_Guild and its helpers until those protocols are rewritten as source".
- The shim defers `Load_Guild_Libraries` while the bytecode executes; the source version calls it at line 2 of its own accord.

## 🎯 Desired outcome

`Grimoire_of_Guilds.py` is the 65edc0f source (with the spec/body split kept: `GuildKit.py` stays the re-exporting spec). Seed 42 is still Nikolas Amexafa; the 78-cell and wide sweeps are unchanged; the replay is exact. The shim, and the Player path's dependency on the vault, are gone.
