## 🔍 Diagnosis (what & where)

Adding a Species today touches seven places:

1. the package or file: Form, Traits as bases, `Set_Physiology` Imprint *(right)*;
2. a declaration Pin, `Player_Handbook_2024(Elf, weight=…, size_options=…, speed=…, description=…)` *(right; needs the `@Pin` mark and keyword-only inputs under TagKit 0.2.0a3, QST-0093.5)*;
3. one import line in `catalog.py`, the explicit load boundary *(acceptable)*;
4. re-export lines in `SpeciesKit/__init__.py` *(a public alias; acceptable)*;
5. the hand-written dispatch in `resolution.py`: `Resolve_Aasimar_Features`, `Resolve_Dwarf_Features`, seven of them;
6. the legacy `AtlasLusoris/Map_of_Species` dict, still read by `Map_of_Character_Generation` for validation;
7. flavour keyed by the species name in 14 files outside SpeciesKit: titles, stories, materials, languages, the Cleric prayers, the spell lodge.

A Species without a Nomina corpus falls silently to a last-resort name.

## 🧾 Evidence

- `grep -rlw Goliath Atlas* app | grep -v SpeciesKit` → 14 files.
- `SpeciesKit/resolution.py` lines 100 to 114: seven imports and seven calls, one per Species.
- Dry run under TagKit 0.2.0a3 with the Pin pattern marked `@Pin`: `Available[:]` reads the catalogue, Heritages inherit the Species' Reports, the root is refused by name (QST-0093 evidence).

## 🎯 Desired outcome

One file per Species (a package when it has Heritages), and one import line in `catalog.py`. The file declares its Form, its Traits, its Kinship, its physiology and its generator metadata as Reports on the Tag; a Source base Tag (`Players_Handbook_2024`, `Homebrew`) supplies the source Reports by inheritance. The catalogue stays the Pin Field `Available[:]`. Resolution is an Operation on the Species Tag, so `resolution.py` dispatches through `Current_Species(character).Resolve(character)` and names no Species. Flavour asks Kinship Flags. A Species names its corpus (`NAMES`) so the fallback is explicit.

## 🧭 Notes for the Agora / implementer

- Depends on QST-0093.5 (the pin moves to 0.2.0a3, which marks the declaration Pins `@Pin`). Decide the rest of the shape here.
- Delete `Map_of_Species` and read `Species_Choices()`.
- Heritages inherit the Species' Reports through `(tag, inherited)` and override only what differs.
