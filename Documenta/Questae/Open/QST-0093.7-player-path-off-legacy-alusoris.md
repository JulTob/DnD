## 🔍 Diagnosis (what & where)

After a full-Guild summon, five modules of the legacy top-level `AtlasAlusoris/` are loaded, through seven shared modules on the Player path. Not one of them builds an NPC:

| Importer | Imports | Used as |
|---|---|---|
| `AtlasActorLudi/Grimoire_of_AbilityScores.py` | `Map_of_Races.AS_racial_modifier` | one call on a 2024 Character (no racial modifiers in 2024; expected no-op) |
| `AtlasInventarium/Grimoire_of_Weapons.py`, `Lodge_of_Basic_Weapons.py`, `AtlasPugna/Map_of_Attacks.py`, `Map_of_Legendary_Actions.py` | `Grimoire_of_NPC.NPC` | a type in annotations and hints |
| `AtlasNomina/Map_of_Names.py`, `AtlasLudus/Map_of_Languages.py`, `Map_of_Useful_Functions.py` | `Map_of_NPC` (`generate_npcs`, `NPC`) | helpers only NPC code calls |

`AtlasLusoris/Map_of_Species.py` also imports the legacy `Map_of_Races` and is itself a parallel species list (QST-0091.1).

## 🎯 Desired outcome

`import app.main` followed by a summon of every Guild loads zero `AtlasAlusoris.*` modules. The `NPC` hints become `Character`; the racial-modifier call is retired once shown a no-op for every 2024 Species; the name and language helpers move beside their only callers; `Map_of_Species` is replaced by `Species_Choices()`. The legacy tree is then NPC-only and waits for the unpark untouched, which keeps Decree 0004's "do not merge" while removing it from the product.

## 🧭 Notes for the Agora / implementer

- Exit test in the sweep script: assert no `AtlasAlusoris` in `sys.modules` after the matrix.
- This is a fold, not a deletion; nothing under `AtlasAlusoris/` is removed here.
