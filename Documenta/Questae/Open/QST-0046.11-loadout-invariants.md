## 🔍 Diagnosis (what & where)

`GearKit._sweep_invariants` generates 7 guilds × 5 levels and checks each
Character. Invariants added for the three-tier and worn-wealth work:

- The three tiers **partition** what is owned: nothing in two tiers, nothing in
  none.
- The belt respects `carry_capacity`.
- Leftover coin above walking money must be worn as jewellery, **if** an unworn
  affordable piece and a free slot both exist.
- No duplicate valuables worn.

**A lesson worth keeping.** The jewellery invariant was first written *stricter
than the rule it guarded*: it asked "is any valuable affordable?" while the rule
also forbids duplicates. It failed a Monk and a Wizard who had correctly finished
shopping. An invariant that does not encode the same rule as the code produces
false failures, which is worse than no invariant, because it trains people to
edit the test.

## 🧾 Evidence

- `python -m AtlasInventarium.GearKit` today → `FAILED — 10 equipment invariant

- That is **not** a gear failure. It is the duplicate-chip invariant from

  looks like markup leaking into a chip label.

deserves minting as its own questa: something is emitting a chip whose label is a
bare `<`, on Bard at every level from 1 to 10. Suspect an HTML fragment reaching
a place that expects a plain label — compare the three doors in QST-0012
(`prose` / `html_prose` / `text_html`).
