## 🔍 Diagnosis (what & where)

Julio: *"we should add a 'carrying' field for an extra weapon or item. Maybe
fighters may even carry one of these for each weapon mastery so they can access
all of them. Makes sense? Multislot entries, basically."*

Before this there were two states: equipped, or in the bag. A Fighter who drilled
six masteries bought six weapons and then buried five of them in a pack, which
made the mastery lines refer to nothing the player could reach.

`Carried` is a Tag, for the same reason `Equipped` is: *"how many things is this
hero carrying?"* stays a question you can **ask**, and `reconcile` can answer it.
The three tiers now partition what a hero owns.

- `carry_capacity` = `CARRY_BASE` (2) + one per Weapon Mastery, so the belt
  widens exactly for the hero the drills were bought for.
- `equip` and `carry` each strip the other, so nothing is ever in two tiers.
- An overfull belt **stows** the excess rather than selling it. An untidy belt is
  not an impossible loadout, and nothing a hero paid for is thrown away for want
  of a hook. (Contrast `SLOTS`, where two breastplates *is* impossible and the
  worse one is sold.)
- `Loadout` gained `carrying`, `carry_room`, `bag` (the pack proper) and
  `stored`. Note `carried()` still means "not in use" for backwards
  compatibility, and `bagged()` is the narrower one.

## 🧾 Evidence

- `Grimoire_of_Items` self-test green today, including the three-tier partition
  and the "an overfull belt must not sell anything" assertion.
- `Carried`, `carry`, `stow`, `carrying`, `bagged`, `carry_capacity` all present.

## 🎯 Desired outcome

Already met. Recorded so the *reasons* survive: the capacity formula and the
stow-don't-sell rule both look arbitrary without them.

## 🧭 Notes for the Agora / implementer

`carry_capacity` reads `mastery_count` through a `try/except`, so a Character
without the masteries module still gets the base 2. That is deliberate: the gear
layer must not hard-depend on the training layer.
