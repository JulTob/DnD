## 🔍 Diagnosis (what & where)

The gates exist but only as a habit: `make smoke-player` is in the Makefile and the pre-push hook; the 78-cell sweep and the wide 1,164-request sweep (every Guild at every level, every Species, Background and Specialization, three genders, each summoned, projected and rendered) live in a scratch directory; `scripts/verify_player_replay.py` is in the repo but in no target. Nothing runs on GitHub. A remote session, or a cloud agent, cannot prove a change without a person at this keyboard.

## 🧾 Evidence

- `Makefile` targets: `run`, `setup`, `smoke-player`, `safepoint`, `install-hooks`, `loss-check`. No sweep, no replay.
- `ls .github` → no such directory.
- The wide sweep found QST-0092 on its first run; the 78-cell gate had passed that Guild.

## 🎯 Desired outcome

- `scripts/sweep_player.py`: the wide matrix as one command, exit code 1 on any failure, signatures grouped; a `--quick` flag for the 78-cell matrix.
- `make sweep-player` and `make replay-player`; the pre-push hook runs the quick sweep after the smoke.
- `.github/workflows/player-gate.yml` runs compile, smoke, the URL suite, the sheet self-test, the replay and the quick sweep on every push to `main` and every pull request; the wide sweep on `main` only (it takes about nine minutes).
- Branch protection on `main` (no force-push, gate required) only on Julio's word: it changes a repository setting.

## 🧭 Notes for the Agora / implementer

- The workflow draft from 2026-09-06 is the starting point; TagKit installs from the pinned commit through `requirements.txt`, so CI needs no secret.
- Python 3.14 on the runner; if `actions/setup-python` lacks it on the day, 3.13 is the fallback and the Makefile already accepts it.
- Kit self-tests run one per process (the double-registration cost from Dialog 0024); a `make selftest-kits` that loops over them in subprocesses belongs here too, once station 1 lands so FighterKit's own test stops refusing its second registration.
