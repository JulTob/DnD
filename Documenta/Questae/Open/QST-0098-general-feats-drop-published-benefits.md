# QST-0098 — Four General feats silently drop a published benefit

- **Type:** bug / rule-fidelity
- **Priority:** 🟠 high
- **Status:** Open
- **Owner:** unclaimed
- **Route to:** Lorekeeper · Technical Team
- **Related:** QST-0067 · `AtlasLusoris/AtlasOfFeats/Map_of_General_Feats.py`

---

## 🔍 Diagnosis (what & where)

Four General feats print less than the published feat grants. None states an
intent to change the rule, so each is a defect rather than a house rule.

| Feat | Missing |
|---|---|
| **Athlete** | The Climb Speed equal to your Speed. Only the stand-from-Prone and running-jump benefits survive. |
| **Dual Wielder** | Quick Draw: drawing or stowing two weapons that lack Two-Handed whenever you would draw or stow one. The surviving clause also says "a different one-handed weapon" where the published text does not. |
| **Medium Armor Master** | The Stealth benefit, which removes Medium armour's Disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth). The armour-training prerequisite is absent too. |
| **Ritual Caster** | The ritual casting itself. The text says only that the spells are always prepared, which on a Character with no Spellcasting feature grants nothing usable. |

## 🧾 Evidence

Audited against the 2024 feat text, entry by entry. Every other number in each
of the four is correct, so this is omission rather than misreading.

## 🎯 Desired outcome

1. Each feat's text carries the full published benefit set.
2. Where a benefit needs mechanics rather than prose (Athlete's Climb Speed,
   Medium Armor Master's Stealth), it is applied and not merely described.
3. If any omission turns out to be deliberate, it is recorded as a 📒 with its
   reasoning at the site, the way Skilled and Shadow Cast are.
