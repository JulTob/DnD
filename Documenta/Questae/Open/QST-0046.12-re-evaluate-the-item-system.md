## 🔍 Diagnosis (what & where)

Julio, 2026-09-07: *"we should record everything in the development process and
evaluate again the item system when we have some moment of clarity."*

The item system was built fast across one long session, then partly lost. Several
decisions were made under pressure, in service of an immediate example, and have
never been reviewed as a whole. This questa exists so that review is **scheduled
rather than improvised**, and so nobody re-opens these questions piecemeal while
the tree is still being repaired.

Questions worth the second look:

1. **Is `Item` still "almost nothing"?** It has accreted `title`, `grants`,
   `attack_with`, `quantity`. Each was justified; the sum may not be.
2. **Crafts vs. wonders vs. implements.** Three ways for an item to be special.
   Are they three ideas or one idea wearing three hats?
3. **The weighting constants.** `_CULTURAL_BUDGET` 12, `_GENERIC_WEIGHT` 4,
   `_TRADE_WEIGHT` 16, `_CULTURAL_WEIGHT` 2 in materials. These were tuned to
   pass specific tests. Do they still express the intent, or the tests?
4. **Balance tables.** `_GUILD_GOLD`, `_BACKGROUND_GOLD`, `_LEVEL_STIPEND`,
   `_WALKING_MONEY` are all provisional and all still tables. The standing plan
   is a `GOLD` Report on the chassis (QST-0046.9).
5. **The "Defender" question**, never resolved: should weapons be able to carry
   warding crafts at all, or is that a shield's job?
6. **`str_requirement` and `stealth_disadvantage`** are recorded on armour and
   enforced nowhere.
7. **`WEAPON_MASTERIES`** is duplicated between a dict and the ledger.
8. **`Grimoire_of_Objects`** is the pre-TOP layer and is still on disk.
9. **Naming.** `ItemKit` vs `Grimoire_of_Items` (see Notes) is unresolved and
   currently inverted.

## 🎯 Desired outcome

A Dialog in the Agora, with Julio, that either confirms each decision or replaces
it — and closes the QST-0046.x family.

## 🧭 Notes for the Agora / implementer

**Precondition: do not start this until the tree is green.** Reviewing a design
through a half-restored codebase produces conclusions about the damage, not the
design.

The naming inversion is worth flagging now: the Conventions table makes `ItemKit`
the right home (*a Kit is "the preferred home for a stable domain object and its
related Tags"*), and the pre-accident tree had renamed `Grimoire_of_Items` →
`ItemKit`. Recovery restored the body onto `Grimoire_of_Items` and left `ItemKit`
as a 17-line re-export, which is the rename **backwards**. It is cross-lane — it
touches every importer — so it wants a decision, not a unilateral flip.
