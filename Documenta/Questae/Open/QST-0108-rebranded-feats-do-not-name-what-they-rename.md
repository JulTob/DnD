# QST-0108 — Ten rebranded Origin feats never name the feat they rename

## 🔍 Diagnosis (what & where)

Ten setting Origin feats are published faction feats with the faction removed
and the name chosen for the background: Agitator, Bastion, Banner Bearer, Field
Lieutenant, Arcane Conduit, Strong Arm, Spared, Cupbearer, On a Roll, Jinx.

The mechanics are kept exactly and each carries a design comment explaining the
choice. None states its published name anywhere a reader can see it, so somebody
holding the published book cannot find the feat they are looking at.

The folder's own convention exists for this case:

> 📙 **Cupbearer** [renames **Vampire's Plaything**]: the rule.

## 🎯 Desired outcome

1. Each of the ten states the published name it renames, in the bracket form.
2. Decide whether the sheet carries it or only the wiki. The bracket is a
   translation key for a reader with the book, which argues for the sheet.
3. The design comments stay where they are; they are the best writing in the
   feat layer and they are the record of why each name was chosen.
