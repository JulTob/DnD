# QST-0109 — Field Marshal has no 2024 equivalent, and grants uncapped Advantage

- **Type:** design / rule-fidelity
- **Priority:** 🟠 high
- **Status:** Open
- **Owner:** unclaimed
- **Route to:** Julio · Lorekeeper · Design-Team
- **Related:** QST-0101 · `AtlasLusoris/AtlasOfFeats/Map_of_General_Feats.py`

---

## 🔍 Diagnosis (what & where)

**Field Marshal** is the only General feat in the catalogue with no published
2024 equivalent. It is the level-4 half of the Field Lieutenant Origin feat,
which is itself a rebrand of a published faction feat.

What it grants:

- 2d6 plus an ability modifier in temporary hit points at 30 feet, proficiency
  bonus times per Long Rest, and
- **Advantage on attack rolls while you are Bloodied**, with no cap, no action
  cost and no duration.

No published General feat grants standing Advantage on attack rolls. That is the
part worth a decision rather than a fix.

It is also the forty-fourth entry in a file the wiki described as holding
forty-three, so any count that quotes forty-three is stale.

## 🎯 Desired outcome

This is a design question, not a bug report. Three ways to settle it:

1. **Keep it as a declared house rule**, marked 📒, with the reasoning recorded
   and the Advantage clause bounded (once per turn, or an action to trigger).
2. **Keep the temporary hit points and cut the Advantage**, which brings it
   inside the published power band without losing the fantasy.
3. **Retire it**, since the Origin feat it extends already exists.

Whichever is chosen, it needs a Decree, because an invented General feat is the
one place the catalogue departs from the published set on purpose.
