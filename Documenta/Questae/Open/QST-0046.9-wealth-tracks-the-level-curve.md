## 🔍 Diagnosis (what & where)

Julio: *"the money cost should reflect the 'money gained per level' we added. We
want to equip them according to their level, not to make them god tier."*

The flat per-level band failed in **both** directions: a level 20 Fighter could
not afford Plate (1,500 gp) while a level 3 one was already flush. Adventuring
wealth compounds by tier, so `_LEVEL_STIPEND` now does too:

| Levels | Per level |
|---|---|
| 2–4 | 25–60 |
| 5–10 | 50–120 |
| 11–16 | 100–220 |
| 17–20 | 180–320 |

Calibrated to buy the best **mundane** kit near the top (roughly 2,700 gp by
level 20) and no more. Magic is left to `Ledger_of_Wonders`, which gates its own
tiers by level.

Also restored here: the **background** contribution, which the legacy layer had
and the first TOP draft silently dropped. Noble 60 down to Hermit 10. Julio had
asked for guild *and* background to shape the kit.

Level 1 gets the baselines only, no level roll — the old table always added one,
so even a first-level character was handed extra coin.

## 🧾 Evidence

- `_LEVEL_STIPEND`, `_stipend_band`, `_BACKGROUND_GOLD` present in `GearKit`.
- Spot check across levels 1/5/12/20 gave level-appropriate kits with purses
  down to walking money.

## 🎯 Desired outcome

Already met. Recorded because the tier numbers are a **balance decision**, and a
future edit that flattens them will not fail any test.

## 🧭 Notes for the Agora / implementer

`_GUILD_GOLD` and `_BACKGROUND_GOLD` are provisional tables with a standing
PENDING note: they should migrate to a `GOLD` Report on the Guild and Background
chassis so the tables disappear.
