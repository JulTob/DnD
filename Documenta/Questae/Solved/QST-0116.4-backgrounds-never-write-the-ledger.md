## 🔍 Diagnosis (what & where)

`AtlasLusoris/BackgroundKit.py` never calls `Commit_Training_Gain`. A Background's Skills and Tool go only to the mutable sheet, through `set_proficiency()` in `_grant_skills` (`:571`, write at `:604`) and `_grant_tool` (`:663`, write at `:712`).

The ledger's queries see them only through the bridge described in QST-0116.3, so every hole in that bridge is a hole in what the ledger knows about Backgrounds. Feature training (Crafter, Musician, Skilled, Skillful) already commits to the ledger, so a Character's training lives in two stores that disagree.

## 🧾 Evidence

On `main` at `34992b6`: `grep -c Commit_Training_Gain AtlasLusoris/BackgroundKit.py` returns `0`. QST-0047 (`:84-85`) already lists `_grant_skills` and `_grant_tool` first among the grant sites to migrate.

## 🎯 Desired outcome

Background training reaches the ledger the same way Feature training does.
