## 🔍 Diagnosis (what & where)

`AtlasInventarium/ToolsKit.py:318` defines `Woodworker_Tools`, and `:370-371` and `:1361-1362` alias the old keys `Carpenter_Tools` and `Woodcarver_Tools` to it. The mutable sheet `Char_Skills` has only the two old attributes (`AtlasActorLudi/Grimoire_of_Skills.py:349`, `:362`) and no `Woodworker_Tools`.

A second legacy sheet, `AtlasActorLudi/Grimoire_of_Training.py:201` and `:214`, holds the same two old attributes.

This is Repairs-Ledger A4, which had no questa.
