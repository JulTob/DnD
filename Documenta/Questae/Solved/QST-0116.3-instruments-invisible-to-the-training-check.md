## 🔍 Diagnosis (what & where)

`AtlasActorLudi/ProficiencyKit.py:307`: `_Legacy_Training_Rank`, the bridge that lets the training ledger read the old mutable sheet, returns `None` whenever `legacy_attribute != key`.

Every musical instrument and gaming set has that shape: its `legacy_attribute` is the category (`Musical_Instrument`, `Gaming_Set`) and its key is the item (`Lute`, `Dice_Set`). So `Is_Trained` never sees an instrument or gaming set that was granted on the sheet, and anything that asks `Is_Trained` before granting one can grant it twice.

## 🧾 Evidence

On `main` at `34992b6`: 14 of 14 instrument and gaming-set definitions have `legacy_attribute != key`.

## 🎯 Desired outcome

`Is_Trained` sees every capability a Character holds, however it was granted.

## 🧭 Notes for the Agora / implementer

- Option raised in the review: Dialog 0025, option C.

## ✅ Resolution (filled when Solved)
- **Decided by:** Julio, in chat, 2026-09-14: "Unify all woodworkers and fix the issues with tools"; then "we are moving forward collapsing the tools... Fix instruments and game sets, unify all tools and its proficiencies (the cool versions with the Works of Wonders and the informative descriptions). Delete duplications." Decided directly; Dialog 0025 closed without deliberation.
- **What changed:** the bridge stays as it is, because "a Musical Instrument" names no instrument and cannot honestly answer for a Lute. Instead nothing grants a bare kind any more: a Background whose Tool is a category draws one kind into the training ledger (a Shawm, a Dice Set), the Bard's Guild already commits three named instruments, and Musician names its own. The sheet lists every instrument and game by name, the Musicianship and Gambling Practices list them, and a Character carries a kit of each kind (the one learned first), priced as that instrument or game.
- **Practice/preference to remember:** grant the thing, not the category.
