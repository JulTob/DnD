## 🔍 Diagnosis (what & where)

Before the 2026-08-29 wipe a Background declared its Hook as an `Entry(title,
definition)`. `_grant_hook` granted it as its own sheet entry with source
`"Background Hook"`, and `_describe` filled slots such as the Scribe's
`{guild}` when the sheet was read. The recovery dropped all three:

- `Build_Background` has no `hook`, and `_awaken` grants one entry only.
- `Grimoire_of_Backgrounds.Background` has no `hook`; the custom hooks are glued
  to the description as `roleplay`. The module still imports `Entry`, unused,
  which is the trace of the field it lost.
- No slot is filled, so the settled Scribe text would print a literal `{guild}`.

The sheet never lost its side: `character_sheet.py` still routes
`"Background Hook"` into a Hook section that nothing fills.

## 🧾 Evidence

Disassembly of `.recovery-vault/_prewipe-pyc-mirror/AtlasLusoris/BackgroundKit.29bfd8e2057f.pyc`
(source 2026-08-26): `_grant_narrative(char, title, description)` grants
`name=title, description=_describe(description), source="Background",
narrative=True`; `_grant_hook(char, hook)` grants `name=hook.title,
description=hook.definition, source="Background Hook", narrative=True`;
`_background_slots(char)` returns `{"guild": char_class or "guild"}`.

## 🎯 Desired outcome

Any Background may declare a Hook, and the sheet prints it as its own titled
entry, with its slots resolved. Nothing changes for a Background that declares
none.

## 🧭 Notes for the Agora / implementer

Architecture only (Julio, 2026-09-14: "architecture by itself"). The data moves
in QST-0122.2 (officials) and QST-0122.3 (custom).

## ✅ Resolution (filled when Solved)
- **Decided by:** Julio, in chat, 2026-09-14
- **What changed:**
  - `BackgroundKit.Build_Background(hook: Entry | None = None)`, reported as
    `HOOK`; construction refuses a Hook without a title and text.
  - `_background_slots` and `_describe`, as before the wipe: a description with
    a `{slot}` becomes a projection resolved when the sheet is read.
  - `_grant_narrative` projects the description and marks the entry narrative;
    `_grant_hook` grants the Hook as `"Background Hook"`, narrative.
  - `Grimoire_of_Backgrounds.Background.hook`; `roleplay` stays until every
    record declares its Hook.
  - `OfficialBackgroundsKit.Register_Backgrounds` passes `hook=record.hook`.
  - `_test_hook_and_slots` in the BackgroundKit self-test.
- **Practice/preference to remember:** the text is data and the Hook is an
  entry: never parse a title out of prose at render time.
