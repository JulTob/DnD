## 🔍 Diagnosis (what & where)

The same list is written out by hand three times, in three formats:

| File | Entries | Written as |
|---|---:|---|
| `AtlasInventarium/ToolsKit.py:971` | 16 | `Tool_Definition` objects |
| `AtlasLusoris/BackgroundKit.py:69` | 17 | string keys, still listing both `Carpenter_Tools` and `Woodcarver_Tools` |
| `AtlasLusoris/AtlasOfGuilds/FighterKit.py:454` | 16 | display names (`"Alchemist's Supplies"`) |

Both old keys resolve to Woodworker's Tools, so the Artisan Background's menu offers 17 entries for 16 tools and draws Woodworker's Tools twice as often as any other.

The only written guidance points at the wrong copy: `Curia/Questae/Open/QST-0047-skills-and-tools-as-top-tags.md:82` says tool names "are the `ARTISAN_TOOLS` tuple in `AtlasLusoris/BackgroundKit.py`. Neither should be re-invented."

## 🧾 Evidence

On `main` at `34992b6`: 16 `Tool_Definition`, 17 `str` and 16 display-name entries respectively; `TOOLS_BY_KEY["Carpenter_Tools"]` and `TOOLS_BY_KEY["Woodcarver_Tools"]` are both `Woodworker_Tools` (asserted by `ToolsKit.py:1431-1432`).

## 🎯 Desired outcome

The artisan tool list is authored once. Every other list derives from it, and the guidance names that one.

- Options raised in the review: Dialog 0025, options B (derive, don't copy) and E (correct QST-0047:82).

## ✅ Resolution (filled when Solved)
- **Decided by:** Julio, in chat, 2026-09-14: "Unify all woodworkers and fix the issues with tools"; then "we are moving forward collapsing the tools... Fix instruments and game sets, unify all tools and its proficiencies (the cool versions with the Works of Wonders and the informative descriptions). Delete duplications." Decided directly; Dialog 0025 closed without deliberation.
- **What changed:** `BackgroundKit.ARTISAN_TOOLS` and `FighterKit.ARTISAN_TOOLS` derive from `ToolsKit.ARTISAN_TOOLS` (keys and display names). Four more copies turned up and went the same way: `Ledger_of_Gear` held a second list of every tool that nothing imported (deleted); `Ledger_of_Tools` now builds each kit from its ToolsKit definition, the Practice flavour as its description; `GearKit._proficient_tools` and `get_other_proficiencies` read ToolsKit instead of hand-kept pairs; `Grimoire_of_Training` (an unimported copy of the skill sheet) and `setObjects` (the retired second equipment pass, with its own tool table) are deleted. QST-0047:82 now names ToolsKit.
- **Practice/preference to remember:** derive, don't copy.
