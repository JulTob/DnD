## 🔍 Diagnosis (what & where)

Julio: *"it would be cool to add 'materials', like 'obsidian sword' or 'silver
katana' that also speak to the culture: Dwarves may focus more on metals, and
gnomes on jewels."* Later, that the legend markers exist *"for items like Mithril
and such."*

`Map_of_Materials._CULTURAL_MATERIALS` is keyed by the **same** markers as
`Map_of_Gear_Titles._CULTURES`, so one species row drives both the name and the
substance, and fixing a species fixes both at once. `_LEAN` adds extra weight for
Dwarves toward metals and Gnomes toward jewels: a bias, never a closed door.

Two constraints that shaped the vocabulary:

- Every entry must read sensibly on **a blade, a haft and a coat**, because the
  `{material}` placeholder does not know what it is filling. Gems therefore
  appear as settings (*"emerald-set steel"*), never as whole objects.
- Materials are **flavour only**: no price, no weight, no grant. The moment a
  material should be lighter or stronger it stops being a describing word and
  belongs in the Ledger as its own item.

**It is broken right now.** `Map_of_Materials` imports `_CULTURES` from
`Map_of_Gear_Titles`, and the bytecode shim **skips `_`-private names**, so the
import raises. The failure reads like a missing symbol and is really a shim
artefact. Any module importing a private from a shimmed module has this same
latent break.

## 🧾 Evidence

- `python -m AtlasInventarium.Map_of_Materials` →
  `ImportError: cannot import name '_CULTURES' from … Map_of_Gear_Titles`.
- 36 marker vocabularies present in source; `_LEAN` and `cultural_materials()`
  survived the loss intact.

## 🎯 Desired outcome

Self-test green again, with the coverage assertion intact: every marker a species
can hold must have materials, or a hero reaches for a substance that does not
exist.

## 🧭 Notes for the Agora / implementer

Resolves itself when QST-0093.2 lands the real source. Do **not** "fix" it by
copying `_CULTURES` into `Map_of_Materials`: two copies of the species mapping is
exactly the duplication the double mapping was built to avoid.
