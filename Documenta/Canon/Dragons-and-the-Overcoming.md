# 🐉 Dragons and the Ascending — why a dragon is a state, not a species

*Julio's organising principle for dragons and dragonborn, recorded 2026-08-14.
**Deep lore, not page text.** In-world the mechanism is forgotten, garbled or
kept quiet, and that is part of the design. This exists so that everything
written about dragons, dragonborn, dragon cultists and Ascendant Dragon monks
stays consistent. Source: the wiki pages `Ǝ.Dr.-Dragons` and `Ǝ.ɖ-Dragonborns`.*

---

## The principle

**A dragon is not a kind of creature. It is something a person becomes.**

A person carries the potential to overcome the culture that made them and
arrive at a self that is genuinely their own. When that happens the change is
not spiritual only: **realisation actualises the body.** What they understood
themselves to be, they become.

Two traditions meet here and say the same thing from opposite ends. Daoist
*ziran*, the self-so, the sage who needs no rule because nothing in them pulls
against the way. And Nietzsche's *become what you are*: the overcoming of
inherited values by someone who can finally author their own.

**The word for it is Ascending.** Literal when they fly off. It is the same
word the Way of the Ascendant Dragon monks use, and they mean it.

## Anyone can Ascend

Not a bloodline, not a species, not a reward. A dwarf who spends ninety years
on one gem may Ascend through the obsession itself, because the obsession was
the only thing in that life that was truly theirs. **Seen from outside this
looks like a curse**, and the family will describe it that way.

**The shape is inherited even when the self is not.** An Ascending produces the
dragon that person was always carrying, assembled from eons of myth and
everything they ever thought a dragon was: a wyrm, a drake, a kirin, a Long, a
winged and impossible lizard. No two traditions produce the same silhouette and
none of them is the real one. The self is authored; the vocabulary of the self
is not.

That loop runs both ways, and it is the same shape as the elves' collective
dreaming. *A single sighting births generations of myth. The story changes the
people who tell it, and that story changes the next dragon born out of it.*

## A dragon is not good

Transcendence is not a moral achievement. An Ascended dragon owes nothing, and
is not kind in any way that serves others. They simply live without friction.

- **The hoard is the meaning**, not the gold. Blueprints, burnt books, oaths,
  choices not taken. It is the one honest possession, being the only thing not
  inherited.
- **Some come back down.** A dragon whose real self is interested in politics,
  or a guild, or a bloodline, will resume a humanoid shape and run one. The
  shape is a tool once you are past needing it to be a self.
- **They resist godhood**, because gods are bound by the wills of others and a
  dragon is bound only by its own. They get worshipped anyway.

Five positions recur, per the wiki: **Promethean** (fire belongs to all),
**Mainländer** (liberation from illusion), **Laozian** (parables and weather
rather than swords), **Nietzschean** (become what you are), and **Mythopoeic**
(the story is everything).

## The religions around it, and why they are not a failure

This is the part to get right, and the earlier draft got it wrong.

Dragon-worship is **not a cargo cult**. The correct parallel is Christianity
and Buddhism: neither rarely produces another Joshua or another Siddhartha, and
both are full of people imitating a transcendence they have not had. Both also
produced schisms, wars and tensions. **That is not a filter placed between
mortals and illumination. It is simply the tragic general condition of any
living religion**, and it does not make the practice fraudulent or the
practitioners fools.

In this setting the shape is closest to **an imperial cult with a monastic
strand**: observance, hierarchy, and rite at the level of the state, with a
genuine contemplative tradition running through it, and ordinary personal
spirituality alongside both.

**Dragon Cultists** are the failure case, and they fail on the philosophy
rather than on sincerity: a self authored to someone else's specification is
precisely the thing that has to be overcome. They are the movement that
worships the man who despised movements.

## Dragonborn stand closest

Dragonborn descend from dragons, are shaped like them, and live near enough to
speak with them. They built a civilisation of rules by **observing beings who
were following none**, which is why their society is codified, grandiose and
outward-facing: every value arrived by watching somebody else.

The cost is real. Expectation crushes people, and a hardness gets demanded of
their own. So is the good: cohesion, service, a house that will not let you
fall, and the warrior in the garden who watches the trees blossom and does not
need to be anywhere else.

**And they are not the ones who missed it.** Proximity is an advantage. Of
every people in the setting, the dragonborn are arguably best placed to Ascend
for real, because they are nearest the thing and can ask it questions. Whether
any of them does is another matter, and never confirmed on the page.

**Draconic Flight at level 5 is the tell.** An adventuring dragonborn sprouts
wings that the ones who stayed home do not. Leaving, and becoming something of
your own out there, is the first flicker of the real thing: a mini-Ascension,
earned by starting to be true to yourself. Nobody in-world names it that.

## Being misunderstood is load-bearing

Nietzsche has been misread for as long as he has been read, and so is this,
in-world. **Never write a scene in which somebody explains it correctly and is
believed.** A character may suspect. A character may be right. Nobody gets
confirmation.

## The peoples, side by side

| people | principle |
|---|---|
| **Dwarf** | Platonic soul-metal; Sainthood as transcendence into one's own pure substance |
| **Aasimar / Celestial** | Platonic Ideals, prior to gods, fixed and incapable of compromise |
| **Elf** | Jungian collective dreaming, malleable, drifting over centuries |
| **Dragon / Dragonborn** | Daoist *ziran* crossed with Nietzschean self-overcoming; the body follows the realised self |
| **Orc** | the wind path, a route each soul walks rather than a substance it is made of |

They disagree, and none is refuted in-world. The dwarf's metal is **given** and
must be proven; the celestial's Ideal is **fixed** and cannot bend; the elf
drifts by **collective** consent; the dragon **authors itself alone**. See
[Elves and the Dreaming](Elves-and-the-Dreaming.md).

## Rules for anyone writing dragons later

1. **Never explain it on the page.**
2. **A dragon owes nobody anything.** Not a mentor, unless mentoring happens to
   be its special interest.
3. **The dragonborn are not a joke, and not a failure.** Play the dignity, the
   cost and the nearness together.
4. **No east/west split.** Every silhouette is one tradition's answer.
