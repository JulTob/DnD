# 🌙 Elves and the Dreaming — why lineages are cultures

*Julio's organising principle for elves, recorded 2026-08-14. **Deep lore, not
page text.** None of this is stated in the Species entry and none of it should
be: death of the author is welcomed here. It exists so that everything written
about elves stays consistent, not so a player reads it.*

---

## The principle

Elves came out of the Fae, and the Fae are made of dream. Something of that
stayed in them: **an elf is malleable the way a dream is.** A people that lives
somewhere long enough, and loves it long enough, begins to look like it.

That is what the lineages are. **Not genetic variance. Cultural adaptation.**
Dream of the forest for a thousand years and you become a Wood Elf: hairier,
some with little stag horns. Dream of the deep places and you become a Dark
Elf, skin like the stones of home. Dream of the crossings and your ears grow
exquisite. Eevee, over centuries, and never by one elf's choosing.

## It is collective, not personal

The mechanism is **collective drift**, a shared subconscious in the Jungian
sense, not one person's dream made real. No elf wakes up changed because they
had a strong feeling. Every legend the people tell shapes them a little; every
century of living somewhere marks the whole culture; and the individual carries
the result of what their people dreamt, not of what they themselves dreamt.

This is the reason the entries are written in the first person plural. *We*
adapted. *Our* forebears stayed where the trees were. The people is the unit
that changes; the character is what it produced.

## What it quietly explains

- **Trance.** Elves do not sleep because they never entirely left the dreaming.
  They are always in it vestigially, and meditation is enough to reach it. The
  rules text says four hours; it never says why, and it should not.
- **Five lineages from three legends.** The species entry names three wars
  (painted faces, icy ships, ambushers from below). Wood, High and Dark. The
  Fae Elf and the Shadow Elf are the branches that never came across at all,
  which is why their marks are the strangest.
- **Why the Shadow Elf is not a villain.** The Shadow realm is the nightmare
  face of the same dream, not a different substance. See the **Shadow**
  Background, which already established that the Fae are made of dream and
  shadows are made of nightmare.

## Fae, Fata, Shadow: three names, one substance

*Julio, 2026-08-21.* There is no essential difference between them. **The Dream
wears a different face depending on who is looking at it, and mortals named
each face separately because they met it separately.**

- **Fae** is the Dream met as court, glamour and bargain: Titania, Oberon, the
  Archfey a Warlock signs with.
- **Fata** is the same thing met as one old woman in a wood, which is where
  *fairy* comes from in the first place (Latin *fata*, the Fates, through
  *fatare*, to enchant). Baba Yaga is not a different order of being from
  Titania. She is the same substance, encountered alone and at night.
- **Shadow** is the Dream met as nightmare, already recorded above as *the
  nightmare face of the same dream, not a different substance*.

Nothing distinguishes the three except the encounter that named them. A witch
who tests you and an Archfey who watches you are doing one thing.

**What it buys.** A fae speaker can call itself a shadow and be telling the
literal truth while the mortal hears modesty or poetry. That is the intended
effect and it should never be explained. Use it sparingly, and never more than
once in a single piece of text, or it stops being a slip and becomes a lecture.

## Sits alongside the other peoples' metaphysics

Each people has one organising idea, and they are deliberately different
answers to the same question:

| people | principle |
|---|---|
| **Dwarf** | Platonic soul-metal, and Sainthood as transcendence into one's own pure substance |
| **Aasimar / Celestial** | Platonic Ideals, emanations prior to the gods, fixed and incapable of compromise |
| **Elf** | Jungian collective dreaming, malleable and drifting over centuries |
| **Orc** | the wind path, a route each soul walks rather than a substance it is made of |

Two of those are Platonic and one is Jungian, which is the joke: the elves are
the only people whose nature is decided by what everyone agrees to imagine.

## Rules for anyone writing elves later

1. **Never explain this on the page.** A player may work it out from "we
   adapted and became like the woods". They should not be told.
2. **Lineages are cultures.** Never write them as bloodlines, subraces, or
   anything a parent passes down. An elf raised elsewhere drifts elsewhere,
   given centuries.
3. **Change is slow and shared.** No elf transforms in a scene, and no elf
   transforms alone.
