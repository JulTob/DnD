# ⚔️ Fighter: the Post at Dawn, read against the whole setting

> 📖 **In flow.** 2 of 9 chapters are still proposals. 📜 1 · 📚 2 · 📔 4 · 📖 2

*Mythos analysis, 2026-09-08. Design and literary criticism, not page text. No
Dialog exists for the Fighter because none was needed: the class text ships,
all five archetype texts ship with design commentary in `FighterKit.py`, and
nearly every feature carries a line. The dialogs for the other classes cite it
as the bar ("training to beat the limits"; the register is the training memoir,
"a training yard at dawn"). This page reads the fullest class on the roster
against the setting, finds the wells its own sentences point at, and lists the
few repairs. Nothing here is landed.*

**Where the text lives.** Class block: vault bytecode, shipping. Archetypes and
their commentary: `AtlasOfGuilds/FighterKit.py` (Battle Master, Banneret,
Champion, Eldritch Knight, Psi Warrior with its three methods by the Order
axis). Lessons and lines: `AtlasOfTraining/Map_of_Fighter_Training.py`. The
Psi Warrior commentary cites `Documenta/Sources/Jedi`, which no longer exists.
No legend register of its own.

---

## 📚 1. What a Fighter player is handed today

Five sheets (Goliath Champion, Aasimar Battle Master, Human Banneret, Dwarf
Eldritch Knight, Elf Psi Warrior). Every one receives the class text, its
archetype text under a heading, and a line above almost every lesson: *"You
don't stay down. You fight."* on Second Wind, *"Take your time. Take your
chances."* on Improved Critical, *"A blade opens one kind of door. Manners
open the rest."* on Knightly Envoy, and the Banneret's bold-**We** lines
(*"**We** don't stay down."*) that turn the class's second person into the
line's first person plural.

✅ **This is what a finished Guild looks like on the sheet**, and it is the
standard every other page in this series measured against. Three things are
wrong with it, and all three are small (§6).

## 📜 2. The core fantasy, read out of the finished text

*"Nobody gave you this. Everyone else was given something. You were not. You
made yourself."* Then the post: *"You got up at dawn and swung a piece of
metal at a post until your hands split. Then you wrapped them and did it
again."* Then the thesis: *"There is no secret. That is the secret. You did
the boring thing every day for years, so that when the danger comes you are
still standing at the end of it. When Death comes for you, it will not
collect. You don't stay down."*

**The Fighter is repetition as transcendence.** The rules say it more plainly
than any other class: the Fighter's high features are its low features
*again*. Extra Attack at 5, 11 and 20 (four swings). Indomitable at 9, 13 and
17 (three rerolls). Action Surge at 2 and 17 (twice). Second Wind's uses grow
from two to four. Where the Monk's list of limits gets shorter and the
Barbarian's Rage gets wilder, the Fighter's tomorrow is today, done better.
Dialog 0018 drew the line exactly: the Fighter trains to *endure*, the Monk
to be *exempt*. Iron against wind.

| Feature (level) | What it proves |
|---|---|
| Second Wind (1) | Stand back up. The post, applied to a wound. |
| Weapon Mastery (1) | "Every weapon you could lift." Three of them at once, then four. |
| Action Surge (2) | More. The one thing the Fighter is always allowed. |
| Tactical Mind (2) | Second Wind spent on a check: the drill applies off the field. |
| Indomitable (9, 13, 17) | Reroll the failed save, with your level added. The years count. |
| Studied Attacks (13) | A miss teaches. The training never stops, including mid-fight. |
| Extra Attack (5, 11, 20) | The same swing, again, again, again. |
| Epic Boon (19) | The only class whose capstone is a feat: the Fighter's transcendence is *another lesson*. |

**The hard edge** is already in the text: "until your hands split". The
training memoir's cost is boredom and pain, paid daily, and the text does not
romanticise either.

## 📚 3. The wells the text already points at

**The palus** (`rome`, Goliaths). Vegetius, *De re militari*: the recruit
trains with a wooden sword against a six-foot post fixed in the ground,
morning and afternoon, until the strokes are habit. ✅✅ *"You swung a piece of
metal at a post until your hands split"* **is the Roman drill, described by
Vegetius**. The Fighter's class text is Goliath-Roman by accident, and the
Goliath temper ("doing it, and reasoning after"; "authority rests on deed and
physical proof") is the class's temper. The Wizard is the Celestials' native
class through Athens; the Fighter is the Goliaths' through Rome. Two peoples,
two classes, named by their vocabulary.

**The laurel** (`sparta`, `homeric`, Goliaths). The Champion "wants to take
the laurel… with the whole arena chanting your name". The arena is Greek, and
the first games in the literature are the funeral games for Patroclus
(*Iliad* 23): the laurel comes from a funeral. ✅ The Goliath Champion (seed
81) is native, and the Champion's "The others call it luck. You have noticed
it comes most often on the days you did the work" is the class thesis in the
arena's voice.

**Destreza's tretas** (`iberia`, Dwarves). The Spanish school named its
techniques, *tretas*, and its footwork, *compases*, and argued about them in
print for two centuries. ✅ The Battle Master's "Generations of people found
them, argued about them, gave them names, and you learned the names" is
Destreza's literature exactly, per the standing ruling. The **Thirty-Six
Stratagems** (`china`, Fae) are the Eastern half: every one has a name ("Borrow
a corpse to resurrect the soul"), and the Battle Master's *Know Your Enemy* is
Sun Tzu's first rule as a feature.

**The alférez** (`iberia`). The ensign who carried the tercio's banner, sworn
not to let it fall; the tercios held Europe for a century and a half on that
oath. ✅✅ *"A banner is not always a flag… You are that symbol. You do not get
to fall"* is the alférez's oath, and the Banneret is the Dwarf's archetype
before it is anyone's. The Orc's version is the *tug*, the yak-tail standard
of the steppe: a banner that is not a flag, on a people who never held a line.

**The telpochcalli** (`aztec`, Dragonborn). The "house of youth" where every
commoner's son trained: the training society made a school. The Dragonborn
entry's "expectation crushes people, and a hardness gets demanded" is the
telpochcalli's cost, and the Dragonborn Fighter is the class in species
clothing without needing the samurai.

**The Sacred Band** (`carthage`, Humans). The citizen elite of Carthage, who
fought as one body. The Human Banneret's line, on a key nobody uses.

**The Gita** (`india`, reached from the sangha). Arjuna on the chariot, before
the battle, afraid. ✅ The Psi Warrior's closing line, *"the true battle is
internal and precedes the blade"*, is the premise of the Bhagavad Gita, and
its three methods (the Lawful "let the course flow over you", the Chaotic
"you are your own master", the Neutral "become the river") are three yogas.
The commentary's source is a missing folder of Jedi codes; the Gita is older,
on a key the setting holds, and says the same thing with a chariot in it.

**Musashi** (`japan`). "A thousand days of training to develop, ten thousand
days to polish." The Monk page took the Five Rings; the Fighter takes the one
sentence, which is the class text's "ten thousand mornings" before the Monk
borrowed it. Decide who owns it: the Monk page recommended the Monk's class
text trim the beat, because the Fighter said it first.

## 📔 4. The Fighter inside the setting's metaphysics

**The Goliaths' class** (§3). The Order of Things is kept by duty and deed;
the Fighter is deed. A Goliath Fighter needs no reconciliation and the
generator's Goliath Champion proves it.

**The Aasimar fleet.** The Aasimar page found Athens' gear is naval (Trireme
Trident, Peltast Javelin): the Aasimar Fighter is a marine of Salamis, not a
hoplite. The Aasimar Battle Master (seed 83) drew the Hermeticist, and "gold
to the sun, silver to the moon, iron to the red star" is a Battle Master's
table of correspondences: a strategist who believes the maneuvers *mean*
something. ✅ Accident, and good.

**"Metal shapes in the forge. People in the challenge."** The Dwarf prayer is
the Fighter's thesis in the species' mouth. The Dwarf Eldritch Knight (seed
87) drew the Artisan: a smith who *bonds* a weapon (War Bond) is the smith's
signature made a rule. ✅ The Dwarf is the Fighter's second home, through the
alférez and the forge.

**The Elf and Studied Attacks.** "You have seven hundred years to be patient
in." An Elf Fighter with Studied Attacks has been studying the same opponent
for a century; the Fighter's ten thousand mornings are an Elf's short career.
The Psi Warrior Elf (seed 89) in the Chaotic method ("you are your own
master") is a Dream-descended people's child refusing the collective, which
is a story the Elf page should keep.

**The Tiefling and Indomitable.** *"If you fail a saving throw, you can reroll
it with a bonus equal to your Fighter level."* The Tiefling entry: "You have
never done anything. That has never been the relevant fact." ✅ The Tiefling
Fighter's Indomitable is the species' whole life as a rule: the verdict was
wrong, roll again, and this time the years count in your favour.

**The Halfling and the post.** "Nothing bad ever happens to us." The Halfling
Fighter is the one who made something bad happen to themselves on purpose,
every morning, until their hands split. The valley would not understand and
the Fighter would not explain.

**The Gnome and the bag of tricks.** "Anything can be observed, taken apart,
solved and improved." The Eldritch Knight's "What you have is a sequence:
this movement, this word, in this order, and then the thing happens" is a
Gnome's description of a mechanism. ✅ The Gnome Eldritch Knight learned magic
as clockwork, and `clockpunk` hands them a wheel-lock.

**The Renegade's refusal.** "You do not fight like someone who trained. You
fight like someone who cannot afford to lose." ✅ The one background that
contradicts the class text on purpose, and the Renegade Fighter is the story:
the one who trained to look untrained, or the one who trained *after*, and
kept the street's habit of not affording to lose.

## 📔 5. Relationships: backgrounds × Fighter

- **Guardian.** *"You learned to be the thing that does not break."* The
  class's own background; "you drill until standing firm is instinct" is the
  post. ⚠️ Reinforcement so exact it should be nudged apart.
- **Sellsword.** *"Danger became just another day's labor."* ✅ The training
  memoir as a job; "you keep your blade honed and your armor sound" is the
  Fighter's morning.
- **Squire × Banneret.** *"You stood where you were told and did not run."*
  ✅ The Squire who became the banner.
- **Herald × Banneret.** Knightly Envoy: "A blade opens one kind of door.
  Manners open the rest." ✅ The Herald's job in the Banneret's feature. The
  Herald Banneret is the one who walks in alone and is the first blade they
  meet, with a banner.
- **Gambler × Champion.** *"Take your time. Take your chances."* ✅ Improved
  Critical is a wider bet, and "the others call it luck" is the Gambler's
  refrain. "You have been rich. Twice in one night you have been nothing" is
  the arena.
- **Investigator × Battle Master.** *Know Your Enemy*: learn a creature's
  measure by observing it. ✅ "Observe. Record. Eliminate." as a level 7
  feature.
- **Debunker × Eldritch Knight.** *"You are not sure how it works, but it
  works like a charm"* against *"There is always a reasonable explanation."*
  ✅ The Debunker who refuses to explain their own magic.
- **Artisan × Eldritch Knight** (seed 87). War Bond as the smith's signature.
- **Hermeticist × Battle Master** (seed 83). Correspondence as strategy (§4).
- **Renegade.** The refusal (§4).
- **Inquisitor.** *"An instrument does not need to be clean, only sharp."* The
  training memoir's hard edge in another trade.
- **Survivalist, Survivor.** Second Wind: "Rescue never came." "You go
  anyway."
- **Archaeologist.** *"You know when to turn around and leave one."* Tactical
  Shift.
- **Stranger.** *"A way of standing that ends a conversation before it
  begins."* The Fighter's bearing, learned elsewhere.
- **Official backgrounds.** Soldier (*Military Rank*), Guard (*Watcher's
  Eye*), Artisan (*Craftsperson*), Noble (*Position of Privilege*): the
  Fighter draws them most and they are one sentence each (Cleric page §8).
  Three of five sampled Fighters drew one.

## 📔 6. Flags

### ✅ Singular, and to be protected

1. **"There is no secret. That is the secret."** The bar. The Monk's answer
   ("the secret your art keeps") is the roster's best cross-page argument;
   confirm both.
2. **The Banneret's bold We.** A device: the Guild's second person becomes
   the line's first person plural for exactly the features that spend the
   Fighter's own resources on others.
3. **The Psi Warrior's three methods by the Order axis.** The only text on the
   roster that branches on alignment, and it branches on *method*, never
   morality. A device the other classes could learn from (the Zealot's god by
   species is its cousin).
4. **The Eldritch Knight answering the Wizard.** "A wizard once called that a
   bag of tricks. They were right." The two schools of magic, across the
   page.
5. **The palus** (§3): the class text is Vegetius, and the Fighter is the
   Goliaths' class.
6. **The alférez** (§3): the Banneret is Iberian.

### ⚠️ Stock, contradictory, or thin

1. **Three lines pasted three times in source.** War Bond, Eldritch Strike and
   Arcane Charge each open with their italic line repeated thrice
   (`Map_of_Fighter_Training.py` lines 1371, 1404, 1418). A paste error; the
   sheet prints it.
2. **Four lines are pop lyrics or pastiche.** *"Rising up to the challenge"*
   (Remarkable Athlete) and *"Rising up, back in the arena"* (Heroic Warrior)
   are the Survivor song; *"Go the distance"* (Survivor, the level 18
   feature) is the film; *"May the power be in you"* (Psionic Power) is the
   Star Wars line bent. They are off the training memoir's register (a
   memoir does not quote the radio), and lyrics on a product sheet are an
   IP question the Bard page already raised for the rulebook paragraphs.
   Drafts below.
3. **The Weapon Mastery line is the shared filler** ("You feel comfortable
   with the weapons you trained with") on the one Guild whose class text
   says "Every weapon you could lift". The Fighter's should be its own.
4. **`Documenta/Sources/Jedi` does not exist.** The Psi Warrior commentary's
   argument ("one Code in one voice; six authors who agree on nothing")
   rests on a folder that was not restored. Either restore it or point the
   commentary at the Gita (§3).
5. **The Battle Master's maneuvers are one-line rules** with no line of their
   own. Correct as chips; but their *names* are the fantasy, and Destreza and
   the Stratagems both named theirs. A maneuver could carry a culture-keyed
   *alias* the way a weapon carries a culture-keyed title, without touching
   the rule. Over-design unless it is one dictionary; noted, not
   recommended.
6. **The Fighter draws the thinnest official backgrounds most** (§5).
7. **"Ten thousand mornings"** is claimed by both the Fighter and the Monk
   texts (§3). One should own it.

## 📔 12. A Battle Master can fail to generate

Repairs Ledger A4. Student of War's untrained pick (`_untrained_choice` in
`Map_of_Fighter_Training.py`, lines 523 to 575) reads tool attributes off
`char.skills`, which holds no tools; when the seeded shuffle reaches a tool
name before an untrained skill, the Training's Imprint fails and the whole
Character does. Reproduced with a Tiefling Fighter, seed 974460, at levels 13
and 14; about one seed in ninety across two random sweeps. Same family as the
Assassin crash in QST-0092. The page's reading of the Battle Master stands;
the drill has a hole in it.
