# 🕯️ Cleric: the Kept, read against the whole setting

> 🚧 **Draft.** Analysis and proposals, not yet authoritative. Under review.

*Mythos analysis, 2026-09-08. Design and literary criticism, not page text. Builds
on Dialog 0016 (core fantasy settled, on the project's seed: to be seen and watched over
by a parental entity; Domains are parenting styles; being watched over teaches you
to watch over). This page does not re-litigate that. It asks what the watcher is
in each people's cosmology, where the warm register has to earn its warmth, what
the prayer ledger already does that nothing else in the project does, and it
drafts the feature lines the sheet lacks. Nothing here is landed.*

**Where the text lives.** Class block, Domain openings, prayer frames and the
prayer ledger: `AtlasLusoris/Map_of_Cleric_Prayers.py`. Lessons:
`AtlasOfTraining/Map_of_Cleric_Training.py` (rules only, no inspiration lines).
Domains on the roster: Life, Light, Trickery, War. Grave and Knowledge exist in
the ledger and the openings but cannot be generated.

---

## 1. What a Cleric player is handed today

A level 11 Dwarf Life Cleric (seed 4) receives the class block (shipping, warm
scripture, the triple "Maybe"), the Life opening with its em-dash still in it,
a seated prayer, and twenty-five rules paragraphs with no line above any of
them. Dialog 0016's three new openings (Light, Trickery, War) are provisional and
not wired; the sheet still carries the plainer ones ("Light always shows the
way.", "The bar does not move.").

The same sheet drew the **Hermit** background, whose feature reads *"Solitude
gave you time to uncover a truth, method, or mystery that still shapes your
decisions."* That is the 2014 Hermit's "Discovery" in the 2014 register, one
sentence, third-person-flat, beside backgrounds like the Inquisitor that run to
four hundred words. ⚠️ See §8: the official backgrounds are the reference's
weakest members, and they are the ones most players will draw.

## 2. The watcher has a different face for every people

The class block offers three guesses and never resolves them: *a god, your
ancestors, the universe itself*. That is Death of the Author performed in-text,
and it is the right design. But the setting's own canon now says what each
people would *mean* by the guess, and in three cases the answer is a tension
worth protecting.

| People | What "something watching over you" is, in their cosmology | The tension |
|---|---|---|
| **Aasimar** | Not the Ideal. Canon: a Celestial "is not a god, has no cult, and cannot want a worshipper"; an Ideal is "fixed and incapable of compromise". | ✅ **The Aasimar carries a spark of something that cannot love them.** An Aasimar Cleric is looking for the parent the Ideal is not. Justice does not hold your hand; something else does, and the Aasimar has to find out what. This is the best Cleric story on the roster and it is entirely implied by two canon sentences. Never state it. |
| **Dragonborn** | Not the dragon. Canon: "A dragon owes nobody anything… is not kind in any way that serves others… they resist godhood, and get worshipped anyway." The religion is "an imperial cult with a monastic strand". | ✅ The Dragonborn Cleric is the cult's priest and knows the dragon is not listening. So the second guess is theirs: *your ancestors*, the clan, "a house that will not let you fall". The watcher is the clan-order the dragons never had. The prayer ledger already says it: *"Clan. Honor. Duty."* |
| **Goliath** | A Giant. "They are still here, if you know how to look at a mountain." The First Ones "kept the world, having never stopped being it." | The most literal watcher on the roster: the parent is the landscape. *"Each snowflake is small, but it is part of the avalanche."* The Order of Things is the household. |
| **Dwarf** | A Saint or an Ancestor. "A gilded prayer to our Saints and Ancestors will never weaken." Soul-metal is *given and must be proven*. | ✅ The demanding parent is the Dwarf's default: the metal was given so that it could be proven. A Dwarf War Cleric needs no reconciliation. A Dwarf Life Cleric is the interesting one: *"Gold, like life, must be shared and passed on"* against a people who count everything. |
| **Elf** | The Dream, which is collective. "May the dream guide your way." | The only watcher that is *everyone*. An Elf Cleric is watched over by the species' own shared subconscious, and cannot know it (canon: never explain). "Maybe the universe itself" is the Elf's guess, and it is closer to true than they think. |
| **Tiefling** | Something that was declared the enemy. Canon: belief made the Lower Planes lower "by being called it"; the horns once held "a shining flame". | ✅ **A Tiefling Cleric may be kept by the very thing the theological shift condemned**, and the sheet cannot say so. *"Ash remembers the flame."* Hagar in the desert names God *El Roi*, "the God who sees me" (Genesis 16:13): the watcher fantasy, first spoken by an outcast slave woman. That is the Tiefling Cleric's scripture, and it should stay in this page. |
| **Orc** | The storm, and the wind path. "If we fall, we carry on." | The two ledger lines disagree (*the wind rider must master the storm* / *must follow the storm*), which is the War / Life split in the species' own mouth. |
| **Human** | Institutions. "Ask the gods, but trust yourself." "Humans tend to organize, making institutions and orders part of our legacy." | The Human watcher is a *church*, which makes the Human Cleric the only one at risk of the intercessor rival 0016 buried. Watch the Human Cleric's backgrounds for it (Acolyte). |
| **Halfling** | Nobody, and that is the joke. "Nothing bad ever happens to us." | ✅ The Halfling Cleric's watcher has never once been needed until the day they left the valley, and now it is. "One can simply walk anywhere." |
| **Gnome** | The thing carried. "Your grandmother could fit the whole history of your people into a piece of jewellery small enough to swallow." | The watcher as heirloom: the Holy Symbol is the thing that is worth more than it looks. |

**The rule this table suggests.** The class block's three guesses are already
enough, but the *Domain frames* ("In a low hour, a line came back to you") could
seat a species-flavoured guess the way they seat a species-flavoured prayer,
without ever naming the watcher. The ledger is the mechanism; nothing new is
needed. See §5.

## 3. Wells the dialog did not draw from

Dialog 0016 read Psalm 121, the Prodigal Son, Virgil, Hermes and the centurion.
Each of these adds a *hard edge*, which is what the dialog's own caution asked
for ("the seed's failure mode is saccharine").

**Job.** The kept child who is tested, and who says *"Though he slay me, yet
will I trust in him."* ✅ **Already in the ledger** under `("grimdark", "Grave")`
as *"Though Heavens slay me, Heavens I trust."* That is Job 13:15, and it is
the single hardest line in the corpus. It is filed under a legend register and
a Domain that cannot be generated. Move it, or copy it, to the default pool: it
is the warm register's necessary shadow, and every Cleric should be able to
draw it.

**Jonah.** The one who runs from the plan and is carried anyway, by a fish.
"What it is, you are not sure you want to know" is Jonah's exact position. The
Trickery Cleric's shadow: the accomplice parent who *will not let you leave*.

**Hagar.** *El Roi.* The watcher fantasy named first by a slave in the desert,
not by a priest in a temple. The Stranger and Tiefling Clerics' scripture (§2,
§7).

**Athena and Odysseus** (Odyssey 13). She disguises him, helps him lie, and
when he lies *to her* she laughs and calls him a wretch, delighted. The
accomplice parent, and Athens is a Celestial key. ✅ The Trickery Domain's best
mythic image, better than Hermes because Athena is watching a *grown* child
and still covering for him. The proposed Trickery opening ("a straight face
kept for the guards") is Athena at the harbour.

**Krishna and Arjuna.** The charioteer who is the god, and who tells the
warrior to fight. The demanding parent in the Eastern well, already in the
ledger as *"You have a right to the work, never to its fruits"* (india,
Knowledge). That line belongs to War as much as Knowledge.

**Guanyin.** The one who hears the cries of the world. Life in the `china`
well; the ledger's *"The highest good is like water"* is beside her.

**Anansi and Legba.** The `africa` key holds a trickster who owns all the
stories and a gatekeeper who opens the crossroads. A Human Trickery Cleric has
two wells the Greek one does not, and both are about *opening*, which is what
Trickster's Transposition and Dimension Door do.

**The Guadalupe pattern.** The mother who appears to the native on the hill,
not to the bishop in the cathedral. The Dwarf keys are Iberia and Andalus and
the Dwarf metaphysic is Sainthood; the pattern of the *Saint who chose the
wrong person* is the Dwarf Life Cleric's story, and the Prodigal Son's cousin.
Keep it as a well, never a name.

## 4. The theology of the Guilds

Dialog 0016 drew the Cleric/Warlock line as **kept / noticed**. The Barbarian
page added the Zealot as **ridden**. The set is larger, and it is worth
recording as one table so the remaining Guilds are written to *stay distinct*.

| Relation to the greater thing | Guild | The verb | Where the discomfort lives |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Kept** | Cleric | watched over, carried, answered before asking | It might be saccharine; the hard edge has to be written in. |
| **Noticed** | Warlock | hired, used, kept alive for the third act | The terms. |
| **Ridden** | Barbarian (Zealot) | worn, set aside, "you are no longer there" | The self steps out. |
| **Sworn** | Paladin | bound by a word the self gave | The oath outlives the reason for it (thread for the Paladin page). |
| **Marked** | Sorcerer | struck, once; an event that keeps moving | The mark is neither taught nor inherited; it is suffered (Sorcerer page). |
| **Lent** | Druid | "the land itself, which is owed and does the owing" (Primal devotion); Dialog 0017: shapes "lent to you like a friend's coat" | Hospitality, not duty; nobody is the parent. (Seat renamed from *Owed* after Dialog 0017.) |

✅ Six relations, six Guilds, no overlap. The Cleric's seat is the only one
whose greater thing *loves first*. Protect that: if a later Paladin text says
"something watches over you", the Cleric has lost its distinction.

## 5. The prayer ledger is the setting's only polyphonic voice

`Map_of_Cleric_Prayers.py` does something no other file does: it lets the
setting's *real* cultures speak in their own proverbs. The Hávamál for the
Norse ("Cattle die, kinsmen die…"), Nezahualcóyotl for the Aztec ("Not forever
on earth: only a little while here"), Machado for Iberia ("Traveller, there is
no road; you make the road by walking"), Teresa of Ávila and Quevedo for the
Dwarf grave, Dante for the Gnome, Saint-Exupéry, the Gita, a Swahili proverb
for the Halfling. ✅ **This is the Cultural Inspirations brief working exactly
as intended**: "the same mapping should eventually inform names, places,
cuisine, architecture, prayer." Prayer got there first. It is the model for
how every other Guild should touch the culture network, and it should be said
so in the Canon.

Three findings, in order of severity:

1. ⚠️ **Two lines can never be drawn.** `CULTURE_DOMAIN_PRAYERS` still keys
   `("greece", "Knowledge")` ("Nothing in excess") and `("greece", "War")`
   ("We learn by suffering"). QST-0046.4 split `greece` into `athens`,
   `sparta` and `homeric`, and `Map_of_Gear_Titles` now *asserts* the key no
   longer exists. The ledger was not updated. Both lines are Aeschylus and
   Delphi, and both belong to `athens` (Knowledge) and `sparta` or `homeric`
   (War). One-line fix; a real bug.
2. ⚠️ **The new markers have no prayers.** `athens`, `sparta`, `homeric`,
   `vatican`, `sangha` and `crusader` are all absent from the ledger, so the
   two peoples whose identity is the classical world pray in nobody's voice
   but their species line. Candidates, all real and short: *"Know thyself"*
   (already in Knowledge; move to athens), *"Come back with your shield or on
   it"* (sparta, War), *"Always to be best and to excel above others"* (Iliad
   6.208, homeric, War), *"All shall be well"* (Julian of Norwich, vatican,
   Life), *"Hatred is never appeased by hatred"* (Dhammapada 5, sangha, Life),
   *"Deus vult"* left out on purpose (crusader is a legend register, and it
   would name the thing).
3. ⚠️ **Three lines are broken or off-register.** *"War is doesn't ask."*
   (Tiefling, War) is a typo. *"Life is a long battle.."* (Dragonborn, War)
   has a double stop. *"If you can't be a good example, then you'll just have
   to serve as a horrible warning"* (default pool) is Pratchett's line for
   Granny Weatherwax and reads as a joke in a pool whose register is warm
   scripture; it is funny, and it is the wrong Guild (the Bard's, or the
   Trickery pool's, not the default's).

## 6. Relationships: backgrounds × Cleric

- **Survivor.** "Why you, and not the others? … You did not escape. You were
  released." Against "you have been carried farther than you know." ✅ **The
  best pairing on the roster.** The same fact read as horror or as love: the
  Survivor Cleric was spared and cannot decide which of the two things spared
  them. The `Spared` origin feat is the rules text of both readings.
- **Herald.** "They chose you. Out of everyone they could have sent. And you may
  still wonder why." That is "You believe you are meant for something. What it
  is, you are not sure" in secular clothes. ✅ The Herald Cleric carries two
  masters and one of them is silent.
- **Destined.** "You glow. An actual aura of light surrounds you on command!"
  Corona of Light at 17 makes the joke literal. ✅ The Destined Light Cleric is
  Percy Jackson claimed: the child who *knows* the watcher chose them and is
  insufferable about it, and right.
- **Fated × Trickery.** Jinx against "the fall that found the hay cart". ✅ **The
  curse and the accomplice parent are one figure seen from two sides.** Things
  go wrong near you; things go right for you. A Fated Trickery Cleric never
  finds out which of the two the watcher is, and the ledger's *"Normal is an
  illusion. What is normal for the spider is chaos for the fly"* is their
  whole theology.
- **Servant.** "Dear, my cup is empty… and I am thirsty." A watcher that is
  hungry. ✅ The Servant Cleric *chose a different parent*, and the old one still
  writes. The Cleric's "a hand held out before you reached for it" against the
  Servant's "the terms were generous". Two households, one child.
- **Inquisitor × War.** "The gods keep a right hand for blessing and a left hand
  for this. You are the left." "You still believe… and you do not sleep well."
  ✅ The demanding parent's child who did what was demanded. The hardest Cleric
  the generator can produce, and it needs no new text.
- **Exorcist.** "You don't need to be devout. You use what works." Against "still
  you do believe." ✅ Contradiction that is a story: the Exorcist Cleric found
  that the technique works *and* that something is there anyway, and would
  rather it were only the technique.
- **Stranger.** "A temple reassigned to a god this country prefers." ✅ **The
  last priest of a suppressed watcher.** The Long Memory hook makes the Stranger
  Cleric the keeper of "the rites that were made illegal", and Hagar's *El Roi*
  is their line: the god who sees the one nobody else counts.
- **Revolutionary.** The Cleric of the Cause. War Domain's "stand where you said
  you would" beside "every hand that shelters you risks the gallows." The
  liberation reading, and one the class text can bear.
- **Bailiff × War.** "Up close there is no oversight at all." ✅ The Bailiff
  Cleric is watched in the one place nobody else was, and knows it. Guided
  Strike as the correction that came when no magistrate was looking.
- **Debunker.** "There is always a reasonable explanation… It is. But the
  explanations can keep you awake." ✅ The Debunker Cleric believes in the
  watcher the way they believe in gas over the marsh: correctly, and without
  comfort.
- **Squire × Light.** "You served someone the songs are about." The teacher who
  shows the road. Reinforcement; the master as the first watcher.
- **Spirit Medium, Tomb Raider.** Both are Grave Clerics without a Grave Domain
  on the roster. ⚠️ The generator will make Grave-shaped people and hand them
  Life or War. Raise Grave (0016's open question 5) or accept the mismatch as
  story.
- **Gambler × Trickery.** "You get forgiven." The accomplice parent's child at
  the table. ✅ *"Not every closed door is locked."*
- **Guardian × Life.** Reinforcement, fine, obvious.

## 7. Flags

### ✅ Singular, and to be protected

1. **The triple "Maybe."** Never resolve it. §2 shows it already has ten true
   answers.
2. **"It was held out before you reached for it."** The prior hand: the whole
   difference from the Warlock. Every later line must keep the care *prior*.
3. **The prayer ledger** as the setting's polyphony (§5).
4. **"The bar does not move. That is how you know it was set with love."** The
   proposed War closing turns the demanding parent into care in one sentence;
   it is the best of the four Domain texts.
5. **The Aasimar's unloving Ideal, the Dragonborn's unlistening dragon, the
   Tiefling's condemned flame** (§2): three species whose canon makes the
   watcher a *question*, and none of them needs a word of new text.

### ⚠️ Stock, contradictory, or thin

1. **Nothing has lines.** Twenty-five lessons, no inspiration line, the only
   Guild whose *class* voice is finished and whose *features* are bare.
2. **Two dead prayer keys, six silent markers, three broken lines** (§5).
3. **The em-dash** survives in the shipping Life opening and the class block.
4. **The Domain openings on the sheet are the plain pass**, not 0016's.
5. **Grave and Knowledge** exist in every ledger and cannot be generated, while
   the backgrounds keep producing Grave-shaped people (§6).
6. **The Human Cleric drifts toward the intercessor** (§2). The only people
   whose watcher is an institution; the background draw decides whether they
   become a priest, which the canon says a background must never force.
7. **The saccharine risk is real on the sheet today.** The class block has no
   hard edge of its own; the edge lives in the Domains (triage, scorch, the
   unmoving bar). A Cleric whose Domain opening is still the plain pass has no
   edge at all. Job's line in the default pool (§3) is the cheapest fix.

## 8. A note on the official backgrounds

The Hermit's *"Solitude gave you time to uncover a truth, method, or mystery"*
is the 2014 book's sentence. The custom backgrounds (Inquisitor, Gambler,
Servant, Stranger, Investigator, Spirit Medium) are the reference the whole
project is being held to; the sixteen official ones are what most Characters
draw, because they are sixteen. ⚠️ A player who rolls Acolyte beside a player
who rolls Inquisitor is reading two different books. This is out of the
Cleric's scope and in everyone's; it deserves its own page, and the extraction
run alongside this one is the evidence for it.

## 10. Threads to pull in later cycles

- **Paladin**: sworn, not kept. The Paladin text must not say "watched over";
  the oath is the Paladin's parent and it is a word, not a face. The `crusader`
  register (Celestial) and `arthuriana` (Paladin's own) meet there.
- **Warlock**: the theology table (§4) is complete only once the Warlock page
  confirms "noticed" survives the four patrons.
- **Aasimar**: the unloving Ideal (§2). Belongs in the Aasimar page as the
  species' central irony.
- **Official backgrounds**: a page of their own (§8).
- **Cultural Inspirations canon**: record that prayer is the first consumer to
  reach the "names, places, cuisine, prayer" goal, and that every new marker
  needs a prayer or the people go silent.

## 12. What the Kept seat costs

Every caster's price is named by the canon except the Cleric's (Lenses, the six
aspects of magic). The Warlock pays in terms, the Sorcerer
in the body, the Dwarf in proof, the Aasimar in an Ideal that does not care
what they meant. The Cleric is kept, watched over by a parent, and the page
never says what that costs. Three options, in the parent frame Dialog 0016
settled, :

| Option | The cost | Line it would give the class |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Attention** | You are never alone, and never unobserved. The parent's care is the same faculty as the parent's watching; you cannot have one without the other. | *Somebody is always watching over you. That is the whole of the word.* |
| **B. Precedence** | The parent's wish precedes yours. Not overrides: precedes. You will find, again and again, that you wanted what they wanted a moment after they did. | *You wanted this. You are almost sure you wanted it first.* |
| **C. Inheritance** | What is kept is kept for someone. One day the parent's work is yours, whether or not you are ready, and the keeping ends the day you begin to keep. | *They are keeping you for something. You have started to guess what.* |

A is the gentlest and the most Life; B is the most Trickery and the most
honest about prayer; C is War's, and it turns the class toward its level-20
question. None is stated on the sheet as a mechanism; each is one line under
Divine Order or Channel Divinity, in the warm scripture register.
