# 📖 Wizard: the Argument Against Forgetting, read against the whole setting

> 📖 **In flow.** 2 of 9 chapters are still proposals. 📜 1 · 📚 2 · 📔 4 · 📖 2

*Mythos analysis, 2026-09-08. Design and literary criticism, not page text. No
Dialog exists for the Wizard, but the class text is finished and shipping from
the vault, and the register rule names it ("the Wizard reads like the Dao De
Jing"). This page reads that text as the settled fantasy, places the class in
the setting, and drafts what is missing: five tradition texts and the feature
lines. Nothing here is landed.*

**Where the text lives.** Class block: vault bytecode, shipping. The Spellbook
feature: `AtlasOfTraining/Map_of_Spellbooks.py`, drawn per Character by tool
("a birch-bark bundle", "a wax tablet", "a folded map, each road a page"), the
best *feature* prose in the project. Lessons: `Map_of_Wizard_Training.py`
(rules only, no lines). A 2014-era layer in `Map_of_Classes/Training/Wizard.py`
still emits *Expanding and Replacing a Spellbook* beside the 2024 Spellbook.
Traditions on the roster: Abjurer, Diviner, Evoker, Illusionist, Bladesinger.
No tradition texts. Culture: no register of its own; Athens is the Celestials'.

---

## 📚 1. What a Wizard player is handed today

Five sheets (Human Evoker, Elf Bladesinger, Gnome Illusionist, Dwarf Abjurer,
Aasimar Diviner). The class text, in full; a Spellbook paragraph that names
the object ("a wax tablet in a wooden cover, each face a page you have written
over more times than you can count"); then rules with no lines, no tradition
text, *Scholar* saying "Choose one skill", the Bladesinger's "one of
Acrobatics, Athletics, Performance, or Persuasion", and the legacy Spellbook
paragraph printed twice under two headings.

✅ The Wizard is the one Guild whose *feature* prose is ahead of its
subclass prose. The Spellbook is drawn from the tools the Character actually
holds, it stays losable by rule ("a book made of memory or muscle would
quietly hand one Wizard an immunity the others pay for"), and "a page is
always a page" so the rules above it keep meaning what they say. That is the
Feature-Text canon working at its best, and it should be the model for every
Guild's signature object (the Bard's instrument, the Ranger's companion, the
Paladin's steed).

## 📜 2. The core fantasy, read out of the finished text

The class text opens on the Dao De Jing's forty-eighth chapter, inverted:
*"In the pursuit of learning, something is gained every day. In the pursuit
of magic, something is lost every day."* Then: *"No symbol has ever held a
True Name. No wizard ever expected one to… Every page you have filled is one
more argument against the entropy of memory."* Then Korzybski: *"A map is not
a country. So you went out there."* And the close: *"Wonder is what happens
to you in the world. The book is only how you carry the memory of it along."*

**The Wizard is the argument against forgetting.** Not the scholar in the
tower (the text refuses it: "a map is irrelevant to someone who has never
walked the road"), not the seeker of true names (the text refuses that too:
no symbol holds one), but the traveller who *writes it down so it is not
lost*. The rules agree, and the arc is exact:

| Feature (level) | What it proves |
|---|---|
| Spellbook (1) | A losable object. Memory outside the body, at risk. The whole class is built on the possibility of losing it, and the rules say "keep a spare". |
| Ritual Adept (1) | "You must read from the book." Some things are read, not remembered. |
| Arcane Recovery (1) | An hour with the book gives some of the day back. Memory as a well. |
| Scholar (2) | Expertise in one field beside magic. The reading went further than spells. |
| Memorize Spell (5) | A different answer prepared by evening, from the page. |
| Spell Mastery (18) | Two spells cast without a slot, *by heart*. |
| Signature Spells (20) | Two more, always prepared, once free. The book stays closed and the spell arrives anyway. |

✅ **The capstone completes the thesis.** At level 1 the memory is a book that
can burn; at level 20 four spells live in the Wizard and cannot be taken with
the bag. The argument against the entropy of memory is won, four times, which
is what a capstone should be: the class text's last line paid in full.

**A limit the setting confirms.** QST-0050 says Celestials "carry a True Name
that defines and limits them". The Wizard text says no symbol has ever held
one. Not a contradiction: the Wizard is the class that knows what writing
*cannot* do, in a world where True Names exist. The most Athenian sentence in
the corpus ("I know that I know nothing" is already in the Knowledge prayer
pool) is the Wizard's epistemology.

**Register.** The Dao De Jing, by the register rule, and the class text already
performs it: short paragraphs that turn on a paradox, concrete and plain. The
tradition texts should be **chapters** in the same book: each one a paradox
the school lives by, two paragraphs, no proper nouns. The device: *the first
sentence of each tradition text is an aphorism a student could carve over a
door.*

## 📚 3. Wells, on the culture keys the setting already holds

**The peripatetics** (`athens`, Celestials). Aristotle taught walking. The
gear map already hands Athens the *Peripatetic Staff* and the *Lyceum
Staff*. ✅ "A map is not a country. So you went out there" is peripatetic
philosophy, and the Wizard's home vocabulary is the Celestials'. The Aasimar
Wizard is native and therefore flat (Aasimar page); the sangha's renouncer
has a better academy: **Nalanda**, the Buddhist university, on the same
people's third key.

**Gongsun Daniang's sword dance** (`china`, Fae). Du Fu's poem records that
the calligrapher Zhang Xu learned his wild cursive from watching her dance
with the sword. ✅✅ The Bladesinger's *Bladework* lets the sword strike with
Intelligence: **the sword is a pen, and the stroke is the sentence.** An
Eastern anchor for the one martial tradition, on the Iberian-and-Eastern
ruling, and it makes the Bladesinger the Wizard's own class rather than a
Fighter with a book. The Fae Elf Bladesinger is where it lands.

**Thoth, and Setne Khamwas** (`egypt`, Humans). The ibis-headed god of
writing and magic, and the tale of the prince who robbed a tomb for the Book
of Thoth and was haunted until he put it back. ✅ The Human Wizard's god is on
the Human key, and the **Tomb Raider Wizard** has the oldest story of stolen
spellbooks on record.

**The House of Life** (`egypt`). The temple scriptorium where the spells were
copied. The Wizard's "copying the book" rule has an institution.

**The Dao De Jing, chapter 11** (`china`). "We shape clay into a pot, but it is
the emptiness inside that holds whatever we want." ✅ The Illusionist's chapter,
and the register's own book: *Illusory Reality* makes what is not there real
for a minute, and the school is the study of usefulness of what is not.

**Vitruvius** (`rome`, Goliaths). The engineer of the aqueduct and the siege
engine. The Goliath's temper is "doing it, and reasoning after"; the Goliath
Wizard is the one Goliath who reasons first, and the Roman half gives them a
craft: the Goliath Evoker builds the fire the legion needed.

**The ledger** (`iberia`, Dwarves). "Carrying their ledgers and their
grudges." ✅ The Dwarf Wizard's spellbook is a ledger, and the Spellbook map
already draws "a sheaf of steel leaves on a ring, each leaf a page struck
rather than written" for a Dwarf with Smith's Tools. The Bank Templars keep
books; one of them keeps this one.

**The Laozian dragon** (Dragon canon). One of the five dragon positions is
*Laozian*: "parables and weather rather than swords". ✅✅ The Wizard's register
is the dragons' idiom. The Dragonborn entry complains that "Dragons speak in
riddles and proverbs, and we cannot be wasting our lives translating their
so-called wisdom"; the Dragonborn Wizard is the one who does. The clan's
translator, whose own class text reads like the thing the clan refuses to
read.

## 📔 4. The Wizard inside the setting's metaphysics

**The stolen fire against the fire itself.** The Sorcerer page's thread: the
Wizard takes fire (Prometheus), the Sorcerer *is* it. The Wizard text agrees
without saying so: "something is lost every day" is the cost of taking. Keep
the two classes on opposite sides of that sentence.

**Two Spellbook forms agree with two species entries by accident.** The Gnome
entry: "Your grandmother could fit the whole history of your people into a
piece of jewellery small enough to swallow." The Spellbook map, for
Jeweler's Tools: "a necklace of amethysts, each stone a page." The Halfling
entry: "all of it written down in recipe books." The Spellbook map's docstring:
"a cook's are recipes." ✅✅ Nobody planned it; protect it. A Gnome Wizard's
book is the thing the grandmother swallowed, and a Halfling Wizard's is the
valley's only literature.

**The Arcane tradition of the Orders is the Wizard's guild.** "Academy, Cabal,
Athenaeum, Conservatory"; "everything is written down twice and one copy is
destroyed"; "nobody is admitted who cannot demonstrate the work". And the Word
domain's sacrifice, "a copy of everything you write goes to them, including
this", is the one clause a Wizard would refuse, which is the story.

**The Elves' Dream and the book.** Canon: lineages drift on what the people
dream and tell, never written. An Elf Wizard writes down what the species
dreams, and the elders would call that the one thing you should never do.
Never on the page; the Elf page should keep it.

**The Orc and the first book.** The Primal practice is "nothing is written; it
is walked". The Bard page gave the Orc the manaschi, half a million lines by
heart. ✅ The Orc Wizard is the first of their people to write the epic down,
and the manaschi will never forgive them.

**The Tiefling and the carvings.** Canon: "carvings nobody can read any more,
and the ones who can read them will not say." The Wizard has Scholar
(Expertise in History) and the argument against forgetting. ✅ The Tiefling
Wizard is the Character positioned to read the carvings. The canon says never
resolve it; the Wizard page says the Tiefling Wizard is the one who *could*,
and chooses, every session, whether to say.

**The dragon's hoard and the losable book.** The hoard is "the one honest
possession, being the only thing not inherited": "blueprints, burnt books,
oaths, choices not taken." The Wizard's book is the one thing the rules insist
can be lost. A dragon keeps burnt books; a Wizard keeps a spare. Two theories
of memory, and the Ascending is the one that stops needing the page.

## 📔 6. Relationships: backgrounds × Wizard

- **Archaeologist.** *"You are not a scholar who travels. You are a traveller
  who reads."* ✅✅ That sentence is the class text's third paragraph in the
  background's voice. The Archaeologist is the Wizard's own background, and
  "an accurate account of where that cup stood… buys you the rest of your
  life" is the argument against forgetting stated as a career.
- **Hermeticist.** *"Neither Druid nor Wizard knows of these secret arts."*
  ✅ The background says Wizards do not know the Art; a Hermeticist Wizard is
  the one who knows both, and the Alchemist's Supplies spellbook form is
  theirs. "The one thing you still cannot produce" against a class whose
  thesis is that some things cannot be held.
- **Tomb Raider.** Setne and the Book of Thoth (§3). ✅ The oldest stolen
  spellbook. "You have read it aloud anyway, because the alternative was the
  ceiling coming down."
- **Stranger.** *"They are also the last library of a world that is being
  forgotten… None of it was ever written down."* ✅✅ The Stranger Wizard
  writes it down. The argument against the entropy of memory, for a people.
  Scholar's Expertise in History is the feature.
- **Gambler × Diviner** (seed 79). *"You can tell three beats early when a man
  is about to double down."* ✅ Portent is a foretold d20; the Gambler Diviner
  knows the roll before it is rolled and still calls it counting.
- **Investigator × Diviner.** *"Observe. Record. Eliminate."* The reasoner
  who sees the future; Holmes with Portent.
- **Fortune Teller × Diviner.** *"Every so often a vision arrives unbidden and
  turns out true."* Reinforcement: the craft becomes real. Nudge apart.
- **Debunker × Illusionist.** *"There is always a reasonable explanation."*
  ✅ Illusory Reality: the Debunker who can make the illusion real. "There was
  no mask, and your hand kept going."
- **Inquisitor × Abjurer** (seed 77). *"You have seen what comes through when
  a seal fails."* ✅ The Abjurer makes the seals the Inquisitor has seen fail.
  Arcane Ward is a seal on a person.
- **Exorcist × Abjurer.** *"Salt, iron, running water, the right words in the
  right order."* The ward, by liturgy.
- **Squire × Illusionist** (seed 75). *"You served someone the songs are
  about. Maybe an archmage."* ✅ The Squire who carried the archmage's book;
  "In Their Name" and Signature Spells: the two spells were the master's.
- **Dragon Cultist × Evoker** (seed 71). The cult's fire, and "someday you
  will become your own Master": the Wizard who studies the dragons' idiom
  from inside the cult.
- **Naturalist × Bladesinger** (seed 73). Odd, and workable: "you paint fast"
  and the sword that writes.
- **Spirit Medium.** Speak with Dead is a Wizard spell; the necromancer is
  not on the roster. The Medium Wizard is the one who writes down what the
  dead say, which is what they want.
- **Ice Nomad, Vagabond.** The Cartographer's spellbook: "a folded map, each
  road a page and every spell a way back to somewhere you have already
  stood." ✅ The map that is not the country, carried by the ones who walked
  it.
- **Herald.** The Word domain: "a letter in your hand reaches people who
  refuse every other letter."
- **Official backgrounds.** Sage (*Researcher*) and Scribe (*Scholarly
  Insight*) are the Wizard's own officials and one sentence each.

## 📔 7. Flags

### ✅ Singular, and to be protected

1. **The class text.** The only philosophical register on the roster, and it
   earns it: chapter 48 inverted, the map that is not the country, "Wonder is
   what happens to you in the world."
2. **The Spellbook map**: form by tool, losable by rule, a page always a page.
   The model for every Guild's signature object.
3. **The capstone pays the thesis** (§2).
4. **Two species entries agree with two spellbook forms by accident** (§4).
5. **The Wizard speaks the dragons' idiom** (§3): the Laozian position and the
   Dao De Jing register are one thing.
6. **The Archaeologist and the Stranger** as the class's backgrounds (§6).

### ⚠️ Stock, contradictory, or thin

1. **No tradition texts, no lines.**
2. **The legacy Spellbook paragraph prints twice** under two headings, one
   with `source=None`.
3. **Scholar** and **Training in War and Song** are open choices.
4. **An em-dash in Portent.**
5. **The Spellbook form is drawn by tool only.** The Materials map is keyed by
   culture ("Dwarves lean toward metals, Gnomes toward jewels"); a Dwarf's
   steel leaves should be Toledo steel and a Dragonborn's could be obsidian
   tablets or lacquered wood. One more draw, same mechanism.
6. **The Bladesinger reads as a Fighter with a book** until the sword-dance
   anchor is on the page.

## 📖 8. Drafts

*House rules: second person; the Dao De Jing's shape (short, plain, turning on
a paradox); no proper nouns; no em-dashes; no open choices. Device: the first
sentence of each tradition text could be carved over a door. Proposals for
proposals.*

### Abjurer (new)

```
The wall is the door. Nobody ever built a wall except to have somewhere to put one.

You learned the making of walls first, because a spell of protection is a sentence that says no, and no is the first word a language needs. Cast one and a ward stands around you and takes what was coming for you, gladly. Later it stands around a friend. Later still, the magic of others breaks against you like weather on a roof. You are not afraid of magic. You have read too much of it to be afraid.
```

### Diviner (new)

```
To see the future is to spend it. That is the price the sky asks, and it is fair.

You wake each dawn with numbers behind your eyes: rolls that have not been rolled, held for later. Spend one and a friend's failure becomes a success, or an enemy's success a failure, and the world does not notice the seam. You will never know what would have happened on the other road. The other road was never there. The third eye is only the second eye, opened.
```

### Evoker (new)

```
Fire has no opinion. The hand that shapes it does.

You studied the school everyone calls the loud one, and learned that its whole art is restraint: the blast that leaves your friends standing inside it untouched, the cantrip that still bites when it misses, the spell that does exactly what it is told and not a hair more. When the moment asks for all of it, you can give all of it, and pay afterwards. The word for what you do is not power. It is aim.
```

### Illusionist (new)

```
The pot is useful for the emptiness inside it. You studied what is not there.

Your lies need no voice, and they can be made to stay. Give one a body and it will walk and speak in your name, weightless, and take the blow that was meant for you and vanish. At the end of the road there is the thing the school never says aloud, because it sounds like a boast: what was not there can be made to be there, for a minute, if you have looked at it long enough.
```

### Bladesinger (new)

```
The sword is a pen. The stroke is the sentence.

Somewhere a dancer's blade taught a calligrapher his hand, and that is the whole of your school: the body reads what the mind wrote, and the reading is fast. When you sing, the sword goes where the thought goes, the blow lands with the mind behind it, and a spell can buy back the hit you did not take. Two strokes, then a word. A word, then a stroke. There is no armour in it. Armour would be a page you could not turn.
```
