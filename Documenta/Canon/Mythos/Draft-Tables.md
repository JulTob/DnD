## 📔 A. The Orders' penalty ladder (Q-0027)

The backstory workbook grades a broken code Minor, Medium, Major. The Orders
have a Sign and seven Relationship forms and no consequence. One draw per
Order on the Order's Dice; the grade follows the beat the arc reached when the
Sign was broken.

**The shapes, by grade**

| Grade | Shape | Read on the sheet as |
|---|---|---|
| **Minor** | an apology, made in the Order's form; a small sacrifice; a night's vigil | *The Order asks for one thing and it is not much, and you will remember it longer than they will.* |
| **Medium** | a labour named by the Order; a trial before witnesses; a loss of standing you feel every time you are introduced | *You are still one of them. You are not sure they are still yours.* |
| **Major** | excommunication; a curse the Order can lay (a story, never a bloodline); the Sign taken back in front of everyone | *You kept the skills. They kept the name.* |

**The Major, by Domain** (the one line each Order would say while doing it)

| Domain | The Major penalty |
|---|---|
| the Forge | *Everything you made with our mark is unmade or unsigned. Choose which.* |
| Home | *The door closes. It is the only thing we have ever closed on anyone.* |
| the Hunt | *You are what runs now. We will not be quick about it.* |
| Mercy | *We will not touch you. You will find out what that costs.* |
| the Veil | *You keep the face. Nobody will believe it again.* |
| the Road | *Every threshold we keep is a wall to you. Walk around.* |
| the Word | *Your name is taken out of the record. Not struck. Taken.* |
| the Storm | *We stop standing between you and the weather.* |
| the Shield | *You stand in the last rank, alone, until you learn what the rank was for.* |
| the Wall | *You are outside. The Wall does not remember who built it.* |
| the Eye | *We will always know where you are. We will never look.* |
| the Beast | *The kinship is withdrawn. Whatever you kept will know it before you do.* |

## 📔 C. A Celestial NPC is an Ideal and a manner (Q-0019)

The Ideals down, the Descent kinds across. Each cell is the manner the
Ideal takes in that kind: the phrase a Dungeon Master reads before the
statblock. The kind-names mortals use (Seraph, Throne, Archon) are distances,
not kinds; this grid replaces the Monster Manual table in the NPC Celestial
sub-table.

| | **Angel** (arrives with a task) | **Muse** (speaks as an art) | **Constellation** (a pattern from below) | **Star** (a point to steer by) | **Planetar** (the wanderer) | **Seraph** (the Ideal at full burn) | **Sphinx** (asks; does not answer) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Justice** | Delivers the sentence; will not hear the appeal. | History, recited until the guilty flinch. | The circle everyone sees and nobody stands inside. | The one bearing that is always the same from anywhere. | Walks off the map when the court is corrupt. | Everything measured at once; nothing survives the measuring. | Asks what you would have done, and waits. |
| **Mercy** | Arrives after the harm, never before. | A hymn that forgives before the confession. | Seen only from the ground, by the fallen. | Steer by it and you arrive at the wounded. | Wanders the roads where nobody stops. | Forgiveness so total it is unbearable to receive. | Asks whom you have not forgiven. |
| **Sacrifice** | Comes to take one thing and names it. | Tragedy: the ending said aloud at the start. | A broken ring in the sky; everyone knows the missing arc. | The star sailors follow into the storm on purpose. | Leaves something in every town and does not come back for it. | Burns what it touches, itself first. | Asks what you would give, then whether you meant it. |
| **Truth** | Reports what was seen; leaves without opinion. | Astronomy: the sky said exactly. | A pattern that means what it means, whoever is watching. | A fixed point that does not care where you were going. | Moves; is never where the chart says. | Nothing hidden survives the light. | Asks the question you have been avoiding, in your words. |
| **Freedom** | Opens the door and does not wait to see who leaves. | Dance: the Ideal with no words at all. | Never in the same place twice; the pattern is the moving. | Steer by it and you never arrive; that is the point. | The wanderer's own Ideal. Cannot be sent. | Every chain in the room comes off at once, the necessary ones too. | Asks what you would do if no one was watching, then watches. |
| **Beauty** | Arrives to be seen; the task is secondary. | Love poetry: the Ideal as longing. | Everyone looks up; nobody agrees what they see. | The one that is brightest, not the one that is useful. | Ruins are its favourite place. | Unbearable to look at; people do anyway. | Asks what you find beautiful, and judges the answer. |
| **Hope** | Comes at the worst moment and stays only as long as that. | Comedy: the ending that is better than it should be. | Faint by day, the only pattern left at midnight. | The last one out when the sky clouds over. | Turns up in a town that has stopped expecting anyone. | A dawn that does not stop; things wither in it. | Asks what you are still waiting for. |
| **Honor** | Announces itself; refuses the back door. | Epic: the deed told as if it were already old. | A standing flame among the moving stars. | The one that does not twinkle. | Wanders because the oath it kept has no home now. | Every promise in the room becomes due at once. | Asks what you swore, and to whom, and whether they know. |

Read across for an NPC's manner, down for the antagonist shapes the Celestials
page lists: **prevent** (Justice, Truth, Honor) and **redeem** (Mercy, Hope,
Sacrifice), with Freedom and Beauty as the two that do neither on purpose.

## 📖 E. Culture keys for the Orc and the Halfling (README open item)

The gear map (`_CULTURES` in `Map_of_Gear_Titles.py`) gives every playable
people two to four **society** keys and one or two **legend** keys, except the
Orc and the Halfling, who draw from the generic pool. The canon's law: one key
is one culture, never fused; overlap between peoples is by influence, not by
sharing a key. Existing society keys, to avoid collision: japan, aztec, china,
korea, persia, levante, india, oceania, africa, egypt, maghreb, carthage,
norse, rus, mongol, celt, iberia, andalus, italy, germany, switzerland,
athens, vatican, sangha, rome, sparta, homeric, ninja.

Per the Design-Team charter, options with tradeoffs; the phonetic law of the
Orc names ("S would bite the tongue") survives any choice.

**Orc**

| Option | Society keys | Legend key | What it buys | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | `kirghiz` | `western` | The class pages already reached for the berkutchi's eagle, the manaschi's half-million lines and the *nerge* hunt; this key lands them. Distinct from the Elves' `mongol` by nation and by epic (Manas, not the Secret History). The cowboy strand of the name file becomes a legend register (*western*: the drover, the long drive, the standard that is not a flag), not a society. | One living culture carries the whole people; the canon's Tiefling caution about mascots applies, and the Orc's "old hatreds" story engine line must go first. |
| **B** | `scythian`, `kirghiz` | `western` | Two steppes, ancient and living: kurgan gold, the recurve bow, griffins on the horse-gear; and the eagle and the epic. The wind path has two ages. | Two keys to write prayers, materials and nouns for. |
| **C** | `pampa` | `western` | The horse plains of the south: the gaucho's *facón*, the poncho, the *payada* sung in the pulpería, the *rastra* belt of silver coins; a plains-rider culture in the project's own tongue, and no nation's costume. *Martín Fierro* as the legend that sits under `western`. | Leaves the class pages' steppe readings as wells the DM reaches for rather than keys. |

Consequences for any option: four Orc rows in the prayer ledger (the storm
lines exist; the key gives them a voice), materials (felt, horn, sinew; or
rawhide, silver, ombú wood for C), and the `_THEMED_NOUNS` "rustic" theme
already covers the Orc Farmer.

**Halfling**

| Option | Society keys | Legend key | What it buys | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | `provence`, `flanders` | `pastoral` | The name file's French surnames and cuisine land on `provence` (the market, the mistral, lavender, the long lunch); the entry's "wide green valley, the wet spring, the cellar of cheese" lands on `flanders` (the lowland fair, the canal, the wheel of cheese, the brass band). Two societies, both temperate farming, neither fused. `pastoral` is the legend register for the pipe-and-second-breakfast material, kept as story, not society. | Two keys to write for. |
| **B** | `provence` | `pastoral` | One key; the French half of the name file already points at it. | The green-valley register has no society of its own. |
| **C** | `flanders`, `bretagne` | `pastoral` | The wet green lowland and the granite coast: cider, buckwheat, the calvary, the fest-noz; the fiddle the Bard page reached for is Breton. | Drops the Provençal cuisine the surnames already speak. |

Consequences: the food given-names retire (Names page); the surnames stay; the
prayer ledger gains Halfling rows in the valley's voice; materials (oak, wicker,
linen, cheese-cloth, cider-oak).

## 📔 F. Euterpe (settled)

Euterpe holds **Harmony**, a primary Ideal added to the roster. The three
options once drafted here (Mercy's second Muse, the Muse of the Descents, or
unassigned on purpose) are all superseded: none was needed once an Ideal existed
for her to hold.

Two things that decision established and which outlive this section. **Harmony
is primary, not a merger**: Justice does not affect mathematics and Beauty does
not affect a ratio, even when the ratio makes a beautiful sound. And **the
roster is open**, so no document states how many Ideals there are. The Muses are
the principal Celestials of their kind rather than a complete set, so a later
Ideal may arrive with no Muse and that is not a gap.

See **QST-0111** for the code.
