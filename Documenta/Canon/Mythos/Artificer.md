# ⚙️ Artificer: Understanding Handed Over, read against the whole setting

> 🚧 **Draft.** Analysis and proposals, not yet authoritative. Under review.

*Mythos analysis, 2026-09-08. Design and literary criticism, not page text. Builds
on Dialog 0013 (core fantasy settled as "understanding, made solid enough to hand
to someone else"; the register is the maker's log; the kit is distributive, so
Nemo lost to Cyrus Smith). This page does not re-litigate that. It places the
Artificer inside the setting's peoples and Orders, finds the makers on the
culture keys, names the signature-object gap, and drafts the feature lines.
Nothing here is landed.*

**Where the text lives.** Class and specialty paragraphs: Dialog 0013's five
texts, provisional and unwired; `AtlasOfGuilds/ArtificerKit.py` is four bare
calls. Lessons: `AtlasOfTraining/Map_of_Artificer_Training.py` (rules only, no
lines; em-dashes in Armor Model, Steel Defender, Eldritch Cannon; "of your
choice" in every Tools of the Trade). Legend register: `clockpunk`, by Guild,
shared with the Gnome. Source: *Eberron: Forge of the Artificer* (2024).
Specialties on the roster: Alchemist, Armorer, Artillerist, Battle Smith.

---

## 1. What an Artificer player is handed today

Four sheets (Gnome Alchemist, Dwarf Armorer, Human Artillerist, Dragonborn
Battle Smith). No class text, no specialty text, no lines. *Replicate Magic
Item* says "one or more magic items from your known plans" and never names a
plan. *Steel Defender* never says what shape the defender took. Two of the
four drew official one-sentence backgrounds (Sage, Artisan); the other two
drew the Squire and the Servant, both of which turned out to be Artificer
stories (§6).

⚠️ **The Artificer's signature objects are absent from the sheet.** The Wizard
page found the Spellbook map drawing a book per Character by tool; the
Artificer, whose whole fantasy is *the made thing in a friend's hand*, has no
made thing on the page. Replicate Magic Item should name the items the
Character made (the plans exist in the source); the Steel Defender should have
a form; the Eldritch Cannon and the Arcane Armor could carry a material from
the Materials map. Same mechanism as the spellbook, three more consumers.

## 2. The makers on the culture keys

Dialog 0013 read Verne, the Mohists, Zhuge Liang, the gunpowder alchemists,
Paracelsus, Hephaestus by implication, the karakuri. These are the ones on
keys the setting assigns, and they share one thing that the dialog did not
say: **three of them built wings.**

**Daedalus** (`athens`, Aasimar). The Aasimar page has him: the craftsman who
built wings for someone else, and the talaria as the wings he built.

**Abbas ibn Firnas** (`andalus`, Dwarves). Córdoba, ninth century: he covered
himself in feathers, jumped from a height, and glided far enough to be
remembered, and made a planetarium and a water clock besides. ✅✅ The Dwarf
Artificer's flier, on the Dwarf's second key, and a real one.

**Wayland** (`germany`, Gnomes). Völund, the lamed smith who built wings to
escape the king who lamed him. ✅ The Gnome Artificer's flier, on the Gnome's
key. Three peoples, three fliers, one class: **the Artificer is the class
whose myth is flight built by hand**, and the Alchemist's *Flight* elixir
("10 ft Fly Speed, 10 minutes") is the joke version of all three.

**Juanelo Turriano** (`iberia`, Dwarves). Charles V's clockmaker: the
automaton monk that walks and prays, and the *artificio* that lifted the
Tagus a hundred metres into Toledo. ✅ The Dwarf Artificer has a namesake in
the setting's own word: an *artificio* is a machine, in Spanish, and the
class name is Iberian in its bones.

**Imhotep** (`egypt`, Humans). The first named engineer: architect of the
step pyramid, physician, and later a god of medicine. ✅ "A miracle that
repeats is called a method" is his career, and he is Alchemist, Armorer and
Artillerist in one life, on the Human key.

**Hephaestus's tripods** (`homeric`, Goliaths). *Iliad* 18: the god's
tripods roll to the assembly of the gods by themselves, and his golden
handmaidens think. ✅ The Steel Defender's oldest ancestor is Homeric, and the
Goliath Battle Smith builds it. The **Force Ballista** on the Eldritch Cannon
list is a Roman engine by name; the Goliath Artillerist is the legion's
engineer, and Archimedes at Syracuse is the Greek half (the claw, the walls,
Fortified Position).

**The Antikythera mechanism** (`athens`). The clockwork sky, in bronze,
Greek. The Clockwork Sorcerer took Kepler; the Artificer takes the older
machine that was actually built.

**The karakuri and the south-pointing chariot** (`japan`, `china`). The
dialog has the tea-serving automaton; the chariot that always points south is
Zhuge Liang's neighbour. The Dragonborn Battle Smith's defender is a karakuri
before it is a robot.

## 3. The Artificer inside the setting's metaphysics

**The Gnome is the Artificer's people, by refusal.** The Gnome entry: *"the
lenses, the clockwork, the smoking herbs. These were found not by fae magic,
but by curiosity."* ✅✅ The species entry explicitly separates the mechanism
from the Dream. The Gnome left the Feywild to *build*, and `clockpunk` is both
the Gnome's register and the Artificer's, so a Gnome Artificer holds it twice.
The culture brief's dilution rule makes that quieter, not louder, which is
correct: the Gnome Artificer is native, and therefore flat, like the Aasimar
Wizard. The interesting Gnome Artificer is the one who went *back* to the
Feywild with a machine.

**The Dwarf builds their soul-metal into armour.** The Dwarf metaphysic is
"soul-metal, given and proven". The Armorer's thesis is "Strength is a thing
that can be built, and you wear the proof". ✅ The Dwarf Armorer (seed 93)
builds the proof of the given metal and steps inside it; Toledo steel from
the Materials map is the plate. "Metal is holy" and "what you make with your
own hands is yours in a way no gift will ever be" are one sentence in two
mouths, and the second contradicts the first just enough to be a story: the
Dwarf who *made* what the Saints say was given.

**The Dragonborn built a civilisation by observing.** *"They built a
civilisation of rules by observing beings who were following none."* The
Dragonborn are the setting's engineers of society, and the Battle Smith
(seed 97) is the habit made literal: they observed a dragon and built a
friend shaped like one. ✅ Never say the Defender is draconic; draw its form
and let the sheet say "a drake the size of a dog" the way the familiars do.

**The Orders' Forge domain is the Artificer's guild**, and its four facets
are four Artificer lives: the Maker ("finish the work the first hands started
and could not complete"), the Unmaker ("anything built can be taken apart,
and someone must know how"), the Fire Thief ("every craft worth knowing was
stolen from something that wanted to keep it"), and **the Price**: *"the
work always takes something back, and the honest maker names it in
advance."* ✅✅ That last creed is *Soul of Artifice* as doctrine: at 0 Hit
Points the Artificer survives by ending one of their own creations. The
dialog called it "the made gives itself back for the maker"; the Forge domain
had already written the sentence. And the Forge's sacrifice, "nothing you
make may be signed with your own name", is the clause an Artificer would
either refuse or embrace, which is the character.

**The Artificer and the Sorcerer and the Wizard.** Three theories of magic:
the Sorcerer *is* it (marked), the Wizard *remembers* it (the book), the
Artificer *builds* it (the bench). "Magic never answers why. So you never
asked" is the Artificer's line and the Wizard's opposite ("wonder is what
happens to you in the world"). Keep the three on three sides.

**The Elf and the mechanism.** The Dream makes elves malleable; the Gnome
refused the Dream for curiosity. An Elf Artificer is the reverse Gnome: one
of the Dream's own who prefers the machine. The elders' second forbidden act,
after writing things down (Wizard page).

**The Halfling and the recipe.** *"All of it written down in recipe books."*
The Alchemist's elixirs are recipes. ✅ The Halfling Alchemist is the valley's
literature made to brew, and Chemical Mastery ("you have tasted most of them
yourself") is a Halfling's whole approach to food.

**The Tiefling and the hood.** *"Some of us can pass under a hood."* The
Armorer's Infiltrator model (Dampening Field, invisibility at 15) is a hood
built by hand. ✅ The Tiefling Armorer made the thing the species needed and
nobody would give.

## 5. Relationships: backgrounds × Artificer

- **Debunker.** *"A wizard with guano and charcoal is not calling down fire
  but making powder."* ✅✅ The background already describes the Artillerist's
  chemistry, and "the medium's table lifts because of a very strong left
  knee" is the maker's eye. The Debunker Artillerist is the one who explains
  their own cannon correctly and still loves it. And "there was no mask, and
  your hand kept going" is the one thing the bench cannot draw.
- **Exorcist.** *"You have tested all of it. Half the book was wrong. You
  kept the half that wasn't, and you charge for it."* ✅✅ That is the maker's
  log as a trade: failures kept and labelled, "never needed twice". The
  Exorcist is an Artificer by method, and the Exorcist Alchemist's elixirs
  are the half of the book that worked.
- **Squire** (seed 95). *"Greatness has logistics: someone has to feed the
  horses, mend the tack, find the ford."* ✅ The Artificer *is* logistics.
  The Squire who kept the packs dry and then built the engine that holds the
  ground.
- **Servant** (seed 97). *Cupbearer*: "create one Potion of Healing or an
  Antitoxin" each rest. ✅ The Servant Alchemist was already brewing at the
  sideboard, and "you poured" is the elixir handed over.
- **Hermeticist.** *"Neither Druid nor Wizard knows of these secret arts."*
  The Artificer is not named, and the Hermeticist Artificer is the one who
  knows both the correspondences and the mechanism: the alchemist-jeweller
  with a bench. Redundant with the Alchemist in mood; fresh with the Armorer
  ("a stone cut on the right day and set in the right metal").
- **Ice Nomad.** *Nothing Wasted*: "you shape your own sled and tools by
  feel… from bone, driftwood, hide, sinew, or packed snow." ✅ Tinker's Magic
  in the cold; the Artificer with no workshop and no complaint.
- **Fated.** *"You break things. Not carelessly."* ✅ Against "Most of what
  you try fails. You keep the failures too." The Fated Artificer's failures
  are the curse and the method at once, and the Jinx aimed at the bad guys is
  the Artillerist's whole ethic.
- **Archaeologist.** *"You made yourself into the instrument that carries the
  record home."* The maker who is the made thing. The Archaeologist Artificer
  rebuilds what the site was, from the lintel's marks.
- **Tomb Raider.** *"Which slab is a counterweight, which floor is a lie."*
  The mechanism, read from the trap side.
- **Arcane Mutant.** *"You have studied yourself the way a scholar studies a
  manuscript, because nobody else could explain you."* ✅ The self as the
  bench. The Arcane Mutant Artificer's first project is the Arcane Mutant.
- **Naturalist.** Specimens and plates; the Alchemist's reagents from the
  field. "A dragon's fang, a moulted celestial feather" are components.
- **Renegade.** The Armorer's suit as the crew's one asset; "the roofs over
  the market" and Powered Steps.
- **Guardian.** The Armorer's Guardian model, named the same. Reinforcement.
- **Gambler.** "You carry with you a game that grounds you" is the Arcane
  Mutant's; the Gambler Artificer builds the loaded die and never uses it.
- **Bailiff.** Forgery Kit, and a badge that can be made.
- **Official backgrounds.** Sage and Artisan drew on two of four sheets;
  *Craftsperson* is the Artificer's own official background and it is one
  sentence.

## 6. Flags

### ✅ Singular, and to be protected

1. **The class close.** "A marvel locked in a drawer is a draft. A marvel in a
   friend's hand is finished. Your magic is the kind that keeps working while
   you sleep." The distributive thesis, and the Wizard's opposite, as the
   dialog intended.
2. **The Battle Smith's grief line.** "What love builds, hands can rebuild."
   The only class with a repairable loss, and the Orc's first unburied horse.
3. **The Forge domain's Price is Soul of Artifice** (§3). Two Atlases agree
   without having met.
4. **Three fliers on three keys** (§2).
5. **The Debunker and the Exorcist are Artificers by method** (§5).
6. **"Strength is a thing that can be built"** answering the Fighter's
   "Nobody gave you this". Keep the argument across the aisle; 0013 asked.

### ⚠️ Stock, contradictory, or thin

1. **Nothing is wired; no lines.**
2. **No made thing on the sheet** (§1): the plans unnamed, the Defender
   unformed, the cannon and the armour unmaterialled.
3. **Three em-dashes** in rules text and **four "of your choice"** in Tools
   of the Trade.
4. **The Gnome doubles `clockpunk`** and reads flat; by design, but the Gnome
   Artificer should not be the default the generator offers most.
5. **The Alchemist's table** (0013's open question 2): the dialog embraced
   the variance; the sheet still says "Roll… or choose", which is the one
   open choice the rules genuinely keep.
6. **The name.** *Artificer* is Latin (*artifex*, the maker), and *artificio*
   is a machine in Spanish. The Vatican and Rome are Celestial and Goliath
   keys; Iberia is the Dwarf's. the project's rule that the classical peoples name
   the classes puts this one, by its bones, with the Dwarves. Worth a line in
   the names page when it is written.

## 8. Threads to pull in later cycles

- **A signature-object Kit**: spellbook, instrument, companion, familiar,
  Defender, cannon, armour, replicated items. Eight consumers of one mechanism
  (form drawn by tool or culture, material by the Materials map, losable by
  rule).
- **Orders**: the Forge domain as the Artificer's guild; the Price as Soul of
  Artifice.
- **Dwarf page**: Juanelo and Abbas ibn Firnas; *artificio*.
- **Gnome page**: native by refusal of the Dream; the one who went back with a
  machine.
- **Names page**: the classical peoples name the classes; the Artificer's
  bones are Iberian.
