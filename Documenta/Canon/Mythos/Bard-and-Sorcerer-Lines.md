# 🎻 Bard and Sorcerer: lines for the two quietest Guilds

> 🔒 **Settled.** No chapter is still in flow. 🔒 0 · 🧾 0 · 🔎 3

*Wiki entry for the design team. The coverage sweep put the Bard at 10% and
the Sorcerer at 19% of Training features carrying a line, the two lowest of
thirteen, and the Lines Annex showed one drafted line each for them across
forty pages. This page closes that gap: one line per Training feature, in the
register each Guild page settled and Dialog 0015 confirmed. Proposals for
the project's hand, 2026-09-08. The Training maps carry no lines today; each of these
would sit before the rule, italic, as the Fighter's do.*

---

## 🔎 The Bard: the told tale

*Register (Bard page, Dialog 0015): oral performance, a drummed cadence, no
verse. The world listens; your art can move it. The four Colleges keep their
Dialog images: Dance is art that needs no words; Glamour is beauty as command,
echoing the Archfey's "part of the show"; Lore is the collector whose word
cuts; Valor is the deed and the telling as one craft.*

| Feature (level) | Line |
|---|---|
| Bardic Inspiration (1) | *You say one thing at the right time, and somebody else is better for a moment. That is the whole art. The rest is practice.* |

| Expertise (2) | *Two things you do the way other people breathe. Nobody applauds breathing.* |
| Jack of All Trades (2) | *You have done a little of everything, badly, in front of people. It adds up.* |
| Font of Inspiration (5) | *The well refills faster than it should. You have stopped asking whose well it is.* |
| Countercharm (7) | *Somebody else is singing in this room. You sing louder, and truer, and theirs breaks first.* |

## 🔎 The Sorcerer: the magic felt before it is understood

*Register (Sorcerer page): sensation, the body first, the name last; each
Origin ends on what the body is turning into, unnamed. The mark is suffered,
never inherited; the species law is not touched. No "bloodline".*

| Feature (level) | Line |
|---|---|
| Spellcasting (1) | *It was in you before you had a word for it. The words came later, and they are still catching up.* |
| Innate Sorcery (1) | *You stop holding it in. For a minute the world notices.* |
| Font of Magic (2) | *There is more of it than the slots can hold. You have learned to pour between vessels.* |
| Metamagic (2) | *You do not cast the spell the way it is written. You cast it the way it feels.* |
| Sorcerous Restoration (5) | *A little comes back after a short rest. It never left; it was catching its breath.* |
| Sorcery Incarnate (7) | *Two ways at once to bend one spell. The body has stopped asking permission.* |
| Arcane Apotheosis (20) | *Whatever you were becoming, you have arrived. You still do not have the word.* |

## 🔎 Notes

- The Draconic lines never say ancestor or bloodline; the body overlaps, and
  what it is becoming is left unnamed, per the Sorcerer page's species-law
  reading and the Dragons canon ("realisation actualises the body").
- Dialog 0015's open question about Glamour echoing the Archfey poem is
  respected: *Mantle of Majesty*'s "It is not a spell. It is a spell." keeps
  the rhyme without quoting it.
- Words of Creation stays verbal for a Dance Bard, as Dialog 0015 found
  beautiful rather than broken: the line says "very seldom," not "never."
- Wild Magic Surge's table is absent from the sheet (Sorcerer page); the line
  is written for the day it is present.
