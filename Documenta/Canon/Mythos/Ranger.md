# 🏹 Ranger: the One Who Does Not Let Go, read against the whole setting

> 🚧 **Draft.** Analysis and proposals, not yet authoritative. Under review.

*Mythos analysis, 2026-09-08. Design and literary criticism, not page text. No
Dialog exists for the Ranger. The standing register rule fixes it (one
register per class: "the Ranger reads like an adventure novel"), but no text
was ever written to it and none survives in the vault. This page derives the
core fantasy from the rules, places the class inside the setting's peoples,
backgrounds and Orders, and drafts a class text, four archetype texts and the
feature lines. Nothing here is landed.*

**Where the text lives.** Class and archetype paragraphs: *nowhere*. Lessons:
`AtlasOfTraining/Map_of_Ranger_Training.py` (rules only; the only line is the
shared Weapon Mastery filler). A 2014-era layer in `Map_of_Classes/Training/Ranger.py`
still emits *"You adopt the Hunter specialization."* as a feature, and a
second *Hunter's Lore*. Archetypes on the roster: Beast Master, Fey Wanderer,
Gloom Stalker, Hunter. No legend register of its own.

---

## 1. What a Ranger player is handed today

Four sheets (Elf Fey Wanderer, Orc Hunter, Elf Gloom Stalker, Halfling Beast
Master). No class text; each archetype's only prose is *"You adopt the Hunter
specialization."*; three of the four drew official one-sentence backgrounds
(Hermit, Charlatan, Acolyte). The Beast Master's *Primal Companion* says
"Choose a Beast of the Land, Beast of the Sea, or Beast of the Sky", which the
generator was supposed to have chosen, and the companion is the archetype's
entire fantasy. *Otherworldly Glamour* says "of your choice".

⚠️ The Ranger and the Paladin are the two emptiest sheets on the roster, and
the Ranger's is worse in one way: its species entries are already written *for*
it (the Elf's "You have seven hundred years to be patient in, and you only need
one second to shoot"; the Orc's "carry more and go without longer than anyone
who says it"), so the absence of a class text is an absence the species entries
point at.

## 2. The core fantasy: the mark that does not let go

**Candidate A, the warden.** The unthanked watch on the border: Aragorn's
Dúnedain, the ranger of the old wiki line ("hunting the dangers at the edge of
civilization"). Real, and folklore at class level: no core rule imposes a
watch, rewards a border, or names a charge. The same verdict Dialog 0017 gave
the Druid's treaty.

**Candidate B, the explorer.** The one who goes into the blank on the map and
comes back. Hawkeye, "the Pathfinder" by name; Allan Quatermain; Hanno's
*Periplus* down the African coast. This is the adventure-novel register's own
protagonist, and the rules fund it: Deft Explorer (Expertise and two
languages: the traveller who reads), Roving (climb, swim, ten more feet),
Tireless (temporary Hit Points from nowhere and Exhaustion shed on a rest:
the one who does not stop). Supporting current, not spine: it says how you
get there and never why.

**Candidate C, the pursuit.** You name a quarry and the naming does not let
go. The 2024 rules are unambiguous about the spine:

| Feature (level) | What it proves |
|---|---|
| Favored Enemy (1) | Hunter's Mark, free, from the first level. The class opens by *naming* something. |
| Spellcasting (1) | Wisdom: perception. The magic is attention, like the Druid's; the object of attention is the difference. |
| Deft Explorer, Roving, Tireless (2, 6, 10) | Everything the explorer needs, in service of arriving. |
| Relentless Hunter (13) | Damage cannot break your Concentration on the Mark. Pain does not shake it loose. |
| Precise Hunter (17) | Advantage against the marked. You see it better than anything else in the world. |
| Feral Senses (18) | You can find what you cannot see. |
| Foe Slayer (20) | The Mark's die grows. The capstone is the Mark, deeper. |
| Every archetype's level 11 | Bestial Fury, Stalker's Flurry, Superior Hunter's Prey, Fey Reinforcements: the Mark or the strike, compounded. |

The class begins with the Mark and ends with it. **The Ranger is the one who
does not let go.** The explorer's toolkit is *so that you arrive*; the
warden's watch is one of the things you may be walking toward. The Orders map
already wrote the doctrine, in the Hunt domain: *"everything that runs leaves
a line behind it, and a line can be walked to its end"*, and its sacrifice:
*"a hunt you accept is a hunt you finish, whoever the quarry turns out to
be."* ✅ The Ranger's creed exists in the codebase, one Atlas over.

**The hard edge.** The failure mode is Javert, and Ahab, and Ethan Edwards
walking away at the end of *The Searchers*: the pursuit that eats the
pursuer. The class text should carry it, the way the Druid's Sea carries Ahab
and the Barbarian carries Ajax: not as a warning, as the thing that makes the
fantasy cost something.

**Register.** The adventure novel, by the register rule, concrete geography,
weather, the map with an edge, sentences that move forward, and the device
of the chapter break: **each archetype text ends on a sentence that stops
mid-pursuit.** Second person, as everywhere in the house. Distinct from the
Druid, who goes into the wild to *belong to it*; the Ranger goes in to *come
back out with something*.

## 3. Wells, on the culture keys the setting already holds

**Jim Corbett.** The man-eaters of Kumaon: one specific tiger, tracked for
months, named. The best real model for Favored Enemy anywhere in print, and
the only one where the hunter's respect for the quarry is the whole book.

**The *Libro de la montería*** (`iberia`, Dwarves). Alfonso XI's
fourteenth-century manual of the bear and boar hunt in the Iberian mountains,
and Don Juan Manuel's *Libro de la caza*. ✅ The Dwarf Ranger is a *montero*,
and the Iberian well holds a whole hunting literature nobody has spent.

**Andalusi falconry** (`andalus`, Dwarves). The treatises came to Iberia
through al-Andalus. ✅ The Dwarf Beast Master's Beast of the Sky is a falcon,
and the falcon is the one animal the two Dwarf keys share.

**The berkutchi** (steppe by temper, Orcs). The eagle hunters of the Kazakh
and Kyrgyz steppe: a golden eagle on the arm, taken from the nest and
returned to the wild after years. ✅✅ The Orc Beast Master with a golden
eagle is the roster's most legible companion pairing, and the *nerge*, the
great ring hunt of the Mongol armies, is the Hunter's *Horde Breaker* seen
from the horde's side. The Orc Hunter breaks the horde with the horde's own
tactic.

**The matagi** (`japan`, Dragonborn). The winter bear hunters of Tōhoku, with
their own dialect and rites for the killed bear. A codified hunt for a
codified people.

**The Calydonian boar** (`homeric`, Goliaths). Meleager and Atalanta, and the
quarrel over the hide. ✅ Atalanta is the Goliath Hunter: the huntress who
outran every suitor. Sparta's own hunt, the krypteia, is the shadow of the
register and should stay a shadow; the Goliath Gloom Stalker inherits it as
the thing the Goliaths do not talk about.

**Skaði and Ullr** (`norse`, Elves). The huntress on skis who chose a husband
by his feet; the god of the bow. The Elf Ranger has its own gods, which keeps
it from being Legolas.

**Hanno the Navigator** (`carthage`, Humans). The *Periplus*: the voyage down
the west African coast, gorillas and all. ✅ The Human Ranger as the writer of
the first travel account, on a key nobody else uses.

**Artemis.** The huntress, and Actaeon's punisher (the Druid page gave Actaeon
to the Moon; the Ranger gets the goddess). The Wild Hunt, Herne's, is the Fey
Wanderer's and the Gloom Stalker's shared night.

## 4. The Ranger inside the setting's metaphysics

**The Ranger and the Druid.** Both cast from Wisdom; both are attention paid
to the wild. The Druid's attention ends in membership; the Ranger's ends in
*arrival*. The Druid page's field diary and the Ranger's adventure novel are
two books about the same country written by a resident and a traveller. Keep
the distinction in the verbs: the Druid *notices*, the Ranger *marks*.

**The Hunt domain is the Ranger's Order.** Its four facets are four Ranger
lives: the Long Pursuit ("close a pursuit that three generations of the order
have failed to finish"), the Debt Collector ("bring back one who was let go"),
What Should Stay Lost ("reach a buried thing before the people currently
digging for it do"), the Provider ("feed a place that is starving"). ✅ A
Ranger in an Order of the Hunt needs no reconciliation; the Order engine can
mint the Ranger's quarry. See §7.

**Dragon versus giant, and who holds the slaying words.** The culture brief
leaves open who should hold `wyrm_myth`, the *slayer's* register (Wyrmbane,
Dragon Lance, Serpent's Bane), which the dragons currently carry. ✅ The
Ranger is the Guild whose whole chassis is the named quarry, and it has no
legend register. Proposal: the Ranger holds `wyrm_myth` by Guild, the way the
Barbarian holds `sword_and_sorcery` and the Paladin `arthuriana`. The
slayer's words go to the class that marks, without taking them from the
Kobolds, and "the dragon-slaying knight is a Goliath story about a Dragonborn"
gains a third party: the one who tracked it there.

**The Tiefling and the dark that cannot see them.** Umbral Sight: *"While
entirely in Darkness, you are Invisible to any creature that relies on
Darkvision."* The Tiefling entry: *"the guard walks you to the edge of town
to be sure."* ✅✅ The Tiefling Gloom Stalker is the one the things that see
in the dark cannot see, past the edge of the town that walked them out. The
first time in the species' life that not being looked at is a power.

**The Elf and the arrow.** The species entry already carries the Ranger's best
line, and the Elf Ranger is the cliché's landing zone because of it. The
interesting Elf Ranger is the Dark Elf Gloom Stalker ("ambushers who came
from below") or the Fey Wanderer, who signed with the Dream (Warlock page §5)
and came back "a little too charming for the woods".

**The Orc and the Mark.** *"You are called raiders instead of riders. You ride
further, carry more and go without longer than anyone who says it."* Tireless
and Roving are the Orc's species entry as rules. ✅ The Orc Ranger is the
class in species clothing; the Orc Hunter (seed 53, *Horde Breaker*) is the
horde's own hunt.

## 6. Relationships: backgrounds × Ranger

- **Survivor.** *"So you went back. For revenge, and something more… It has
  your face. Every time you have come close it was because you were allowed
  to."* ✅✅ The Survivor Ranger's Mark is on the thing that let them go, and
  Relentless Hunter says damage cannot break it, and the hook says being
  *allowed* can. Javert, Ahab and Corbett in one Character, and the adventure
  novel's best plot.
- **Bailiff.** *"You learned how to find a person who does not want to be
  found."* ✅ Hunter's Mark as a warrant; the Badge and the Mark are two
  authorities and one can be called back.
- **Archaeologist.** *"You have crossed country that kills people who packed
  badly. You have gone through a window at speed with a lamp in your
  teeth."* ✅ Quatermain exactly (*King Solomon's Mines* is an
  archaeologist-ranger novel). Deft Explorer's two languages are "a language
  you started learning two weeks earlier on the road".
- **Stranger.** *"You learned the roads out and the roads back, which
  crossings ask for papers and which guards can be talked past."* ✅ The
  diaspora's guide; the Ranger who knows the way because the way was the
  difference between arriving and not.
- **Exorcist × Fey Wanderer** (seed 51). Beguiling Twist turns a Charm back
  on someone else. ✅ The exorcist who *redirects* possession rather than
  ending it, with spells from the court that does not admit mortals twice.
  "The procedure is much the same whether it is a fiend or a philosophy."
- **Charlatan × Gloom Stalker** (seed 55). The Gloom spell list is Disguise
  Self, Rope Trick, Greater Invisibility, Seeming. ✅ A charlatan's kit, and
  the dice drew the pairing.
- **Survivalist.** The class's own background. ⚠️ Redundant; nudge apart.
- **Vagabond.** *"Chasing one more horizon."* The explorer current, and the
  Ranger who has not yet named a quarry. The adventure novel's drifter.
- **Ice Nomad.** *"The old roads worn into the ice by generations of runners
  and sleds."* The Ranger of the ice; Tireless in the cold.
- **Wildkeeper.** *"A track of dead things leading somewhere nobody goes."*
  The Ranger's errand, delivered by the Druid's background.
- **Guardian.** The warden reading, housed in a background rather than the
  class: "the wall between the weak and the wolves". Fine.
- **Inquisitor.** The seventy-two sealed names: Favored Enemy in the old
  sense, with a book.
- **Naturalist.** *"Half of what you have catalogued has bitten you at least
  once."* The Ranger who brings the specimen back alive.
- **Sellsword.** The bounty. The Mark for hire.
- **Renegade.** *"You cross a district by rooftop faster than a runner takes
  the street."* Roving on slate; the urban Ranger nobody writes.
- **Herald.** "You walk in alone, past the guards." The one who arrives.
- **Dragon Cultist.** With `wyrm_myth` on the Guild (§4): the cultist Ranger
  carrying Wyrmbane is the canon's kinslayer-or-trophy-taker in one kit.

## 7. The Quarry: a line the sheet could draw

The 2024 *Favored Enemy* promises an enemy in its name and delivers a spell in
its rule. ✅ In this setting the sheet can keep the promise without a rule:
draw one **Quarry** line at level 1 from the Hunt domain's goals and the
backgrounds' hooks, seated the way the Cleric's prayer is, and never
mechanical. Examples in the register:

- *There is a pursuit three generations before you failed to finish. You have
  the map they drew, and the last river on it is wrong.*
- *Something was let go, once, by someone who is now dead. You are bringing it
  back.*
- *There is a thing buried that other people are digging for. You intend to
  arrive first.*
- *A valley is starving. Whatever is left in the country to hunt, you will
  find it.*
- *It has your face. Every time you have come close, you were allowed to.*

The line is the adventure novel's first chapter, and it is what the class text
means by "the thing itself".

## 8. Flags

### ✅ Singular, and to be protected

1. **The Hunt domain is the Ranger's creed**, already written (§2, §4).
2. **The species entries already carry the class** (Elf, Orc). Any class text
   must not repeat them.
3. **The Tiefling Gloom Stalker** (§4). Not being looked at, as a power.
4. **The Orc Beast Master's golden eagle** and the nerge (§3).
5. **The adventure novel's device**: every archetype text ends mid-pursuit.

### ⚠️ Stock, contradictory, or thin

1. **Nothing is written.** No class text, no archetype text, one shared
   filler line, and "You adopt the Hunter specialization." as a feature.
2. **The companion is never drawn**, and it is the Beast Master's whole
   fantasy (§5).
3. **Hunter's Lore prints twice** (2024 and legacy).
4. **The Elf Ranger is the cliché's landing zone**; the register and Skaði
   rescue it only if the class text refuses "archer".
5. **Three of four sampled sheets drew one-sentence official backgrounds.**
   The Ranger's natural officials (Guide, Sailor, Wayfarer) are the thin ones.
6. **The Ranger has no legend register**; it is the class that most needs the
   slayer's words (§4).

## 9. Drafts

*House rules: second person; the adventure novel's texture (geography,
weather, the edge of the map); no proper nouns; no em-dashes; no open choices;
the Ranger comes back out with something, never merely belongs. Every
archetype text ends mid-pursuit. Proposals.*

### Class text (new)

```
Every map has an edge where the rivers stop being named. You went past it. Not because anyone sent you, and not to keep a watch nobody thanks you for, though you have done that too. You went because there was a thing out there with your name on it, and you had already given it yours.

That is the craft, under all the rest. You name a quarry and the naming does not let go. Rain does not wash it off. Pain does not shake it loose. You can climb after it, swim after it, walk three days without sleep after it and still have hands steady enough for the shot. Everything else you know, the second language, the reading of ground, the way you can step into a hedge and stop existing, is only so that you arrive.

Others go into the wild to belong to it. You go in to come back out with something. Most days it is a road, or a person, or the way home. One day it will be the thing itself. You are still walking toward it.
```

### Hunter (new)

```
One quarry at a time. You learn what it is made of, where it is soft, what it is afraid of, and after that it does not matter how many of them there are. You were trained to break a horde and step out of it untouched, and to answer a giant's blow with one of your own.

The beast is always bigger than you. You know its name before it knows yours. You have not yet met the one you cannot outlast, which is not the same as saying it does not exist. You will know it when the ground goes quiet.
```

### Beast Master (new)

```
Something walked out of the wild and chose you. Not tamed and not bought. It came, and it stayed, and when you put your mark on a quarry it hunts the mark with you. It fights the way you fight and it falls the way you fall, and when it falls you sit with it through the night and it stands back up.

There is no word a city would understand for what you are to each other. Hunters have one, and do not say it in front of strangers. You have not gone in alone for years. You are not sure you remember how.
```

### Gloom Stalker (new)

```
The dark was never the thing to fear. It was where the frightened things went, and you followed them there and learned the trade. Now you go first, faster than anything expects in the first breath of a fight, and whatever sees in the dark cannot see you at all.

You have looked into the eyes of the thing that hunts at night, and it blinked. It does not know yet that you are still there.
```

### Fey Wanderer (new)

```
You went past the edge of the map and found there was another map under it. Somebody there took a liking to you. You came back a little too charming for the woods, with a step that crosses distances the woods do not have, a way of turning a charm back on whoever cast it, and a friend or two who arrive when you whistle and are not entirely made of anything.

The court has not finished with you. You are fairly sure you have not finished with it.
```
