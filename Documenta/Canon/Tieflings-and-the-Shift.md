# 😈 Tieflings and the Shift — why belief made them what they are

*Julio's organising principle for tieflings, recorded 2026-08-14. **Deep lore,
not page text.** None of this is stated in the Species entry and none of it
should be. It exists so the writing stays consistent, and so nobody later
"clarifies" the entry into allegory.*

---

## The mechanism

The setting already established how Celestials work. From the wiki: *mortals do
not create celestials directly, but their beliefs are the **fuel** from which
celestials emerge*, and *whenever a being makes a choice, they select which
principle they believe in, and a celestial tied to that principle gains power*.

Apply that rule honestly and it runs both ways.

**If belief makes celestials, belief makes fiends.** A theological shift
declares one half of the cosmos the enemy. Everyone believes it, hard enough,
for long enough, and the machinery obliges. The Lower Planes did not
necessarily begin as the Lower Planes; they were made lower by being called it.

**And the proof is retroactive.** The horns that once held a sacred flame
become evidence of damnation. Nothing about the horns changed. The reading did,
and the reading is what the world runs on.

This is the same engine as the Aasimar's, pointed the other way, and that is
the point: the two species are one mechanism seen from opposite ends. See
[Dragons and the Overcoming](Dragons-and-the-Overcoming.md) for how the other
peoples' principles sit alongside it.

## What was there before

Before the shift they were **gifts**. The suggestion the entry allows, and
never explains, is a priesthood: crown-horns holding an eternal flame,
something held up, an office rather than a curse. Egypt is the register, since
it is the one inspiration the setting had not spent.

**All that survives on the page is one sentence about carvings nobody can read
any more**, and the note that the ones who *can* read them will not say. Never
resolve this. A character may find an inscription. A character may translate it
correctly. Nobody in authority confirms it.

The people-without-a-land thread runs underneath: a diaspora with no homeland
to be exiled *from*, only one to be exiled *by*.

## Why there is no culture

**A tiefling is born to ordinary parents.** This is the structural fact that
separates them from every other people in the setting, and everything else
follows from it.

There is no elder, no quarter of the city, no festival where everyone has
horns. Nobody hands down what you are, because the people who made you do not
have it either. Some families hide the child. Some report a stillbirth and mean
it as mercy. A tiefling meets other tieflings by accident, as adults, and has
to invent everything from scratch each generation.

**Formally, this is why the Species entry is the only one with no "we".** Every
other people speaks collectively somewhere: *our own*, *young one*, *we carry
on*. Tieflings get second person alone. The isolation is in the grammar rather
than asserted, and it must stay that way.

## The parallels, and the rule about them

Several real experiences rhyme with this. **None is named on the page, and none
should ever be**, because a species that is explicitly one thing becomes a
mascot and stops being a people.

- **Marked as evil while carrying no evil.** The RAW itself says the legacy
  *"has no effect on the tiefling's moral outlook"*, and the entry takes that
  literally: nothing in the three legacy blocks describes a personality.
- **Marginalised and itinerant**, read as thief and corrupter on sight, beaten
  in a public square while nobody's hands move.
- **Neurodivergence and the social model of disability.** A difference that is
  real, sometimes powerful, and carries no defect in itself, treated as a
  defect by the environment around it. Spanish *minusvalía* is the tell: the
  word means **lesser worth**, so the judgment was written into the name before
  anyone examined the person. That is the theological shift in miniature.

The line that carries all of them at once is **"You have never done anything.
That has never been the relevant fact."**

## Rules for anyone writing tieflings later

1. **No biological determinism, ever.** The legacy does things; it never makes
   anyone a kind of person. Describe what the legacy does and what the body
   looks like, and stop.
2. **Never state a parallel.** No line may be traceable to one real group.
3. **Never resolve the carvings.**
4. **Keep the "we" out.** No collective voice, no homeland, no elders.
5. **The legacy is power, not impairment.** A tiefling is not lessened by what
   they carry. They are lessened by what is decided about it, which is a
   different sentence and the only true one.
